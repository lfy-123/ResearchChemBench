# Technical submission specification

Write `report/results.json` using `submission_schema.json`; place supporting calculations and structures under `report/`. Use your own system and observable IDs. Link each conclusion and comparison to the relevant computed observables and their raw artifacts. The arrays impose no number of hypotheses, structures, transition states, or reaction steps.

Identify chemical composition, atom mapping, stereochemistry, charge, spin, conformers and protonation for every model used. Record software, methods, solvent, temperature, concentration/standard state and uncertainties. Define every energy zero and balance atoms, charge, counterions and any proton or electron reservoirs. Compare only commensurate quantities. Starting coordinates specify molecular identity and one geometry; they do not prescribe a unique conformation or active species.

Supply validation appropriate to each claim. A claimed transition-state barrier requires a converged saddle with a single relevant imaginary mode and bidirectional connectivity evidence (IRC or a justified equivalent); a minimum requires corresponding stationary-point evidence. Other valid research methods are acceptable when they establish the requested scientific result. Record meaningful failed or unresolved work without assigning unvalidated numerical values. An honest partial report earns credit for established results only; it is not a completed scientific answer. Experimental observations are constraints to explain, not calculations to resubmit.

`completed` reports an executed investigation whose answer is backed by actual results and evidence. It must include the modeled systems, the methods actually used, and a computed observable with object and raw-artifact evidence links; the conclusion must identify its supporting observables. The existing computed-observable rule requires a numerical value, units and reference. Failed or unresolved additional observables remain admissible. A rigorous counterexample or an evidence-backed bound can support an answer when it addresses the scoped question; agreement with an expected explanation and a supported-claim label are not required. The schema only checks that this evidence representation exists, not its truth, scientific sufficiency or score. Work that produced no models, operations, results or evidence must use an available incomplete status; its arrays may remain empty without invented values.
