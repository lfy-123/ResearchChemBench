## Scientific objective

What accounts for the relative productive reactivity of the two configurations of substrate S in Pd/L-pyroglutamic-acid C-H olefination, and what can separately defined S/P configurational-interconversion energetics establish about the observed enantioenriched product?

Use `data/inputs/system.json` for the full S/A/P graphs, the chiral ligand, reaction conditions and experimental observations. S starts racemic; P has the specified E alkene. No absolute nitrogen configuration is supplied as an observed assignment, so define a consistent structural mapping of the two configurations.

Develop an evidence-supported explanation of their relative productive reactivity, and investigate configurational interconversion of S and P under the separately stated comparison conditions. Assess how far those results can explain the reaction observations. A change of solvent or temperature changes the comparison. The question does not require a full catalytic cycle, the kinetics of uncharacterized coordinated interconversion, a quantitative dynamic-resolution model, or investigations of other azepines. Conclusions about behavior at 363.15 K in acetonitrile must be limited to what the evidence supports.

State structures, stereochemical mapping, protonation, total charge and multiplicity, solvent, temperature, energy definitions and reference-state bookkeeping. Choose reliability checks appropriate to the claims actually made and distinguish what is supported from what remains unresolved.
