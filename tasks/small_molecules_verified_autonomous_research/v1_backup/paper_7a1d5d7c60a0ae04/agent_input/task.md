## Scientific objective

For hydrogenative reductive cycloetherification of 1,4-diphenylbutane-1,4-dione and 1,5-diphenylpentane-1,5-dione, what evidence-supported molecular account explains catalyst-dependent cis/trans formation selectivity and its relation to product stability across ring sizes, while accounting for the supplied reaction-performance comparisons among the specified triarylboranes?

Develop a computationally supported explanation of the connected catalyst-function and stereochemical comparisons, rather than merely repeating the observed product ratios or reporting one molecular descriptor.

## Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use `data/inputs/system.json`. It supplies neutral molecular identities and relative product stereochemistry (PI1), distinct reaction conditions (PI2), and experimental observations (PI3). The closed-ring descriptions repeat the same oxygen atom only to indicate ring closure.

The stereochemical comparisons are D4 with Catalysts A and B, and D4 versus D5 with Catalyst A held fixed. The K4 screen with Catalysts A, B and C is context for evaluating catalyst function and the limits of a molecular interpretation of reaction performance. It is not a separate demand to discover furan-forming pathways or calculate endpoint yields. Do not extrapolate to unlisted catalyst/substrate pairs, other substrate classes, a full catalytic network, quantitative turnover kinetics, pressure dependence or solvent-screen mechanisms. No experimental work is required.

Keep isolated yields, endpoint NMR yields, measured ratios and lower bounds distinct. The two condition sets are not interchangeable. Any connection between model evidence and an experimental endpoint needs stated assumptions; an endpoint yield is not an activation barrier.

## Required investigation

Formulate testable explanations and choose molecular models, informative computational investigations and appropriate validation. You decide which physical effects and hypotheses warrant testing, which computational methods to use, and how to revise or retain explanations when results arrive. No particular intermediate, descriptor, conformer procedure, mechanism, software package or search sequence is prescribed.

Address the connected scientific relationships: what molecular evidence can and cannot establish about catalyst function and the performance contrasts; catalyst-dependent cis/trans formation competition for D4; and how the D4/D5 ring-size comparison relates product formation selectivity to product stability. A single descriptor, geometry or energy without an evidence-supported account of these relationships does not resolve the question. You need not establish an entire reaction network or exhaustively enumerate conformers or pathways.

Actually execute the chosen research. Preserve real molecular inputs, calculation inputs and outputs, logs and analysis code. Explain model choices, definitions, states, units, conditions and how each investigation informs a conclusion or a decision. Separate observations, computed evidence and interpretations, and report important ambiguity and uncertainty.

Validation follows the claims made: a transition-state claim needs appropriate frequency and reaction-connectivity evidence; a minimum claim needs appropriate stationary-point validation; a kinetic-model claim needs its equations, mapping to observations and identifiability assessment. Such claims and methods are not mandatory. An unvalidated structure or an unexplained numerical result cannot establish the associated scientific claim.
