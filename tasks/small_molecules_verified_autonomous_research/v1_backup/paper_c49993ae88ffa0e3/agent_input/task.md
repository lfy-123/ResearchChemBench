# Scientific question

Investigate the molecular basis and thermodynamic feasibility of light-driven pyridine N-oxide deoxygenation by preformed phenanthrene-9,10-diol in acetonitrile, and assess how local hydration alters the reaction performance. The focus is on the reduction stage using the preformed reagent.

# Inputs and scope

`data/inputs/system.json` contains molecular identities, the parent pyridine N-oxide model, thermochemical conditions and scenarios with zero or two added water molecules. `data/inputs/experimental_observations.json` contains the separately identified experimental systems and observations. The parent molecular model and experimental derivatives are distinct inputs.

The required scope is reduction through products using the preformed reagent. Reagent preparation or regeneration, full catalytic turnover, rates, yields, bulk water-activity fitting and a unique kinetic mechanism are excluded. Supplied inputs and general scientific/software documentation are permitted; private evaluator or computation references, prior run archives and another mode's guidance are excluded.

# Required results

Provide quantitative computational evidence for the reduction through products and the hydration comparison, with an evidence-supported molecular explanation. Report chemical identities, electronic states, composition, conditions and explicitly defined physical references for all quantities. Comparisons require balanced species inventories and recoverable validity evidence. Identify supported and unresolved findings.

# Submission

Submit `report/results.json` and `report/report.md` under `submission_schema.json` and `submission_guide.md`, with native inputs, outputs, execution records and analysis.
