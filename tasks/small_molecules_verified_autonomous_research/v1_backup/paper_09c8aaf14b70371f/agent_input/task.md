# Scientific question

Can a conventional computational chemistry explanation cover the differences in cathodic peak potentials across the five supplied chalcones? At the same time, assess the extent to which a molecular structure–property relationship affects the series and solvent dependence.

# Inputs and scope

`data/inputs/chalcone_series.json` contains complete mapped molecular structures, measurement conditions and observed peak potentials in acetonitrile and DMSO, plus the dichloromethane measurement for compound 3. The supplied starting molecules are neutral E isomers with singlet labels.

# Required results

Provide a calculation-supported electronic-structure-property explanation as well as a molecular-level explanation covering the five compounds and the supplied solvent comparisons, including its supported scope and unresolved findings. Report the identities and electronic states of calculated species, quantity definitions, conditions and references, with recoverable computational evidence.

# Submission

Submit `report/results.json` and `report/report.md` under `submission_schema.json` and `submission_guide.md`.
