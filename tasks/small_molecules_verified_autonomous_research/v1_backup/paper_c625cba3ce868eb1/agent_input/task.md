# Scientific question

Using the observed cyclization of compound 7 under enzymatic conditions as a mechanistic reference (the enzyme is not the principal controller of regioselectivity), investigate which electronic-structure properties and molecular structure–property relationships influence the local ring selectivity of hydroperoxide (R)-4 under the supplied nonenzymatic NBS conditions.

# Inputs and scope

`data/inputs/systems.json` contains the molecular graphs and reagent identities. `data/inputs/source_facts.json` and `data/inputs/observations.json` contain operations, conditions and experimental outcomes. The selected molecule is (R,E)-4.

The NBS experiment used a mixture of hydroperoxides 3 and 4 in DCM; its reported 18% isolated yield belongs to that mixture. No numerical reaction temperature is reported. Compound 7's product distribution was measured under enzyme conditions. Separate calculations on 7 are optional.

The required scope is local molecular selectivity. The enzyme cycle and upstream photochemical preparation are excluded.

# Required results

Provide quantitative computational evidence and a molecular explanation of the observed ring selectivity, with the experimental context of comparisons, the scope supported by the evidence and unresolved findings stated. Report molecular identities, quantity definitions, conditions, references and recoverable validity evidence.
