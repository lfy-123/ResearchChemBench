# Scientific question

Which catalytic processes explain cycloisomerization and intramolecular [2+2] product formation from the supplied 1,7-allenene model and Rh catalyst, and what controls their competition?

# Inputs and scope

`data/inputs/system.json` contains substrate and product graphs, catalyst identity, solvent and concentration conventions. `data/inputs/rhodium_catalyst.xyz` is a catalyst starting geometry. The product graphs define the transformations to explain. The simplified O-tethered substrate has no experimental yield dataset.

The required scope is these two product classes. CO-insertion carbonylation and full experimental catalyst speciation are not required. Supplied public inputs and general scientific/software documentation are permitted; private evaluator or computation references, prior run archives and another mode's guidance are excluded.

# Required results

Provide calculation-supported, chemically connected catalytic pathways, quantitative energetic comparisons on balanced references, and a conclusion about the competition. Report chemical identities, electronic states, conditions, units, reference definitions and recoverable validity evidence for the structures, transformations and kinetic claims submitted. Identify incomplete or unresolved results.

# Submission

Submit `report/results.json` and `report/report.md` under `submission_schema.json` and `submission_guide.md`, with native inputs, outputs, structures, execution records and analysis.
