# Scientific question

Calculate and explain the differing site selectivity of the two-step Mallory photocyclization of precursor 1-cis, and the resulting product connectivity and selectivity.

# Inputs and scope

`data/inputs/precursor_1_cis.xyz` specifies the 52-atom C30H22 neutral singlet precursor and its atom mapping. The coordinates are a starting geometry. No experimental product identity or yield is supplied.

The question concerns isolated-molecule mechanism and selectivity. Explicit solvent, iodine, propylene oxide, crystal packing, excited-state dynamics, rates and device properties are optional and outside the required scope. Only authorized public inputs may be used; private evaluator files, reference calculations and archived answers are excluded.

# Required results

Provide first-stage and sequential second-stage site/connectivity alternatives, their comparable energetic evidence, independent electronic or structural site-selectivity evidence, and the supported sequential route and final product connectivity. Each proposed product or intermediate requires an unambiguous mapping to the precursor. A claimed stationary point requires corresponding stationary-point evidence. Quantity definitions, molecular inventories, conditions, standard states and conformer treatment must be explicit.

# Submission

Submit `report/results.json` under `submission_schema.json`. Include every generated chemically distinct candidate, its stage/connectivity, sequential continuation where applicable, mapping, status and associated calculation/validation evidence. Include the actual research record, coverage, discarded or unresolved candidates and final conclusion. Unavailable quantities must be identified as unavailable. Auxiliary results are separate from the required results.
