# Scientific question

What substrate-centered chemistry accounts for the formation of acetophenone from sec-butylbenzene under the supplied photocatalytic conditions? Provide a quantitative molecular explanation of the local carbon–carbon cleavage and its selectivity.

# Inputs and scope

`data/inputs/molecular_identity.json` contains the starting-material graph and initial geometry, reagent identities and observed product. `data/inputs/reaction_conditions.json` contains experimental conditions and the molecular comparison convention. `data/inputs/experimental_observations.json` contains measured outcomes and measurement definitions.

The required scope is local small-molecule chemistry. A complete photophysical cycle and polymer-degradation mechanism are excluded.

# Required results

Provide calculation-supported local transformations, quantitative comparisons of relevant alternatives and the supported conclusion, with unresolved findings identified. Report molecular identities, electronic states, balanced transformations, energy references and supporting validity evidence.

# Submission

Submit `report/results.json` and `report/report.md` under `submission_schema.json` and `submission_guide.md`.
