## Scientific objective

How can the ligand environment of the specified manganese precatalysts affect plausible active forms and the accessibility of isopropanol oxidation and acetophenone reduction, and what stereochemical preference can be supported for an explicitly defined catalyst configuration?

## Inputs and scope

Use `data/inputs/system.json` for the complete precursor/ligand graphs, base treatment and reaction observations. Propose chemically plausible active-form hypotheses from these starting facts and investigate how they influence the two transfer-hydrogenation half-reactions. No active form or population is supplied as fact. The assessed scientific scope is the reaction energetics of defensible candidate forms and stereochemical outcomes conditional on a specified catalyst configuration. A calculation of precursor activation barriers, proof of actual solution populations, full catalyst speciation, a bulk enantiomeric excess or an experimental rate is not required. The sulfur-free precursor is experimental context. Molecular-H2 hydrogenation, isotope effects and complete precursor equilibria are outside the required core.

## Investigation and evidence

Develop and test explanations for this question. Choose informative models and calculations, execute them, and use the evidence to retain, revise or reject your explanations. The mechanism, structures to investigate, methods and execution sequence are your choices. No exhaustive reaction network or fixed compute quota is required.

Provide actual structures, inputs, outputs, logs and analysis. Identify molecular graphs, conformations, charge, electronic states, medium and thermodynamic conventions. Numerical evidence belongs in `evidence[*].quantities` with a scientific name, physical definition, value, unit and conditions. Distinguish profile ordinates, local activation barriers, reaction free energies and electronic energies; specify the molecular and particle ledger for each difference. A transition-state claim needs frequency and reaction-connectivity evidence. Kinetic or population claims need a defensible mapping from the calculated quantities to the observation.

Scientific credit concerns supported intermediate scientific results and conclusions. Merely restating an experimental observation, copying a source value or completing a calculation job does not establish the result. A well-supported alternative interpretation is acceptable within the question's scope. State limitations and source ambiguities without silently selecting favorable values or assigning uncomputed quantities.

## Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json`, `report/report.md` and `research/` under the shared schema and guide. Link investigations, evidence and conclusions to real artifacts. Record actual settings and resource use. Research completion and question resolution are separate: preserve honest partial or failed work with its attempted inputs and logs. Stop when the evidence supports the stated scope; do not represent unresolved work as a resolved scientific result.
