## Item-by-item criteria

### r_endo_ocm

Recover substrate coordination and endo oxidative cyclometalation to the eta3 organorhodium intermediate using connected, composition- and CO-balanced structures and valid stationary-state evidence. Names INT1/INT2 are source labels, not mandatory output IDs.

### r_overall_barrier

Recover the overall cycloisomerization activation free energy relative to the physical resting-state reference identified as INT11 in the main discussion. Do not substitute the local 12.9 kcal/mol barrier relative to INT1 or unbalanced CO references.

Numerical target: 22.8 ± 2.0 kcal/mol.

### r_co_loss

Establish a valid CO-dissociation connection from the oxidative-cyclometalation intermediate to the next catalyst state. Include the exchanged CO in composition and energy references. A demonstrated equivalent connection is acceptable; do not require the historical failed-IRC/QRC trajectory.

### r_beta_h

Establish the connected beta-H elimination within the paper cycloisomerization channel, with appropriate saddle/mode or equivalent connection evidence and consistent species inventory. The source local 6.0 kcal/mol is context with no new numerical gate.

### r_product_release

Recover reductive elimination leading to the cycloisomerization product and chemically consistent catalyst regeneration/ligand exchange. The source local 8.9 kcal/mol is contextual; do not impose an uncalibrated magnitude target.

### r_competing_22

Recover direct reductive elimination from the OCM intermediate to the distinct [2+2] product with connected molecular evidence and a common energy reference. Product existence alone is insufficient. The local 17.1 kcal/mol is not the overall cycloisomerization barrier.

### r_endo_exo_gap

Compare the correctly assigned endo and exo oxidative-cyclometalation transition structures of the same substrate/catalyst and establish that the endo saddle is lower in Gibbs free energy. Require valid saddle/connectivity evidence and comparable composition, charge/spin, conditions, thermochemical definition and energy reference. This item scores the supported ordering, not agreement with a particular numerical gap; the source 7.5 and SI 7.2 kcal/mol discussions are context only. Comparing unrelated minima or unbalanced states does not establish this ordering.

### r_competition_conclusion

The final selected mechanism uses endo OCM followed by connected cycloisomerization and [2+2] branches, and explains their competing accessibility from a balanced free-energy profile. Endo initiation controls the main route; do not confuse local and overall barriers or infer an experimental product yield from this finite model. No CO-insertion [2+1+2] scope expansion.
