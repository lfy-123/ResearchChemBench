## Item-by-item criteria

### r_acid_trans_connection

Actual molecular structures and reaction-connectivity evidence support C–C bond formation between the specified E-hex-3-enoic acid and an adamantyl electrophilic center, and ring closure through carboxyl oxygen to the supplied five-membered lactone with a trans ethyl/adamantyl relative configuration. The authors' reference is a concerted cyclization TS retaining the acidic proton, with total charge +1 and singlet multiplicity. The reaction mode and local connectivity must correspond to these bond-forming events, and the protonated endpoint must be related to the neutral target product with balanced atoms/protons. Scientifically equivalent connectivity evidence is permitted; a full IRC or deprotonation TS is not mandatory. The supplied product, one correct distance or normal termination alone is insufficient.

### r_sulfonamide_trans_connection

Actual structures and local reaction connectivity support C–C bond formation and five-membered N–C ring closure of the complete specified E-alkenyl sulfonamide, giving the trans ethyl/adamantyl relationship in the supplied N-p-toluenesulfonylpyrrolidine. The authors' TS retains the N–H proton, with total charge +1 and singlet multiplicity; the protonated endpoint must be reasonably related to the neutral product. An unconverged supermolecule cannot be presented as a minimum, but valid reaction modes, finite downhill paths and independently qualified fragments may provide commensurate evidence. A complete catalytic cycle or cis competition is not required.

### r_acid_local_barrier

G(TS1–3a)−G(Ad+)−G(1a)=13.0 kcal/mol, with absolute tolerance 2.0 and inclusive boundaries. Check HFIP, 293.15 K, 1 atm, +1 singlet reacting components and retention of the acidic proton. The TS must have saddle-point/connectivity evidence corresponding to the bond formation. This quantity is not the 15.7 height on the full profile starting from AdOH; do not splice the authors' ionic energy of 2.7 into independently computed local quantities. Reconstruction of solvent thermal corrections must be explicit. Physically equivalent standard-state/unit conversions are permitted; fitted offsets are not accepted.

Numerical target: 13.0 ± 2.0 kcal/mol.

### r_sulfonamide_local_barrier

G(TS1–3s)−G(Ad+)−G(1s)=10.2 kcal/mol, with absolute tolerance 2.0 and inclusive boundaries. Check HFIP, 293.15 K, 1 atm, the complete E-sulfonamide and a +1 singlet TS retaining the N–H proton, with actual saddle-point/connectivity evidence. This quantity is not the 12.9 height on the full profile starting from AdOH. All energies must come from a consistent independently computed ledger; do not mix the authors' ion-formation energy with independently computed local energies. Transparent, physically equivalent conversions are permitted.

Numerical target: 10.2 ± 2.0 kcal/mol.

### r_acid_product_energy

G(trans lactone 3a)+G(H2O)−G(AdOH)−G(E-alkenyl acid 1a)=−12.3 kcal/mol, with absolute tolerance 2.0 and inclusive boundaries. Use valid neutral singlet minima and consistent thermal corrections for HFIP, 293.15 K and 1 atm; TfOH/HFIP cancel as conserved spectator components in the complete ledger. A protonated product, ion-pair energy or arbitrary lower conformer cannot replace this net reaction quantity without an explicit identity account.

Numerical target: -12.3 ± 2.0 kcal/mol.

### r_sulfonamide_product_energy

G(trans N-p-toluenesulfonylpyrrolidine 3s)+G(H2O)−G(AdOH)−G(E-alkenyl sulfonamide 1s)=−12.1 kcal/mol, with absolute tolerance 2.0 and inclusive boundaries. Use valid neutral singlet minima for HFIP, 293.15 K and 1 atm; retain the complete substrate, atom and proton ledger. A cationic endpoint retaining an extra N–H cannot directly replace the neutral net product.

Numerical target: -12.1 ± 2.0 kcal/mol.

### r_trans_pathway_answer

In the final conclusion, use actual connectivity and structural evidence to connect adamantyl electrophilic bond formation and ring closure through carboxyl oxygen or sulfonamide nitrogen to the two supplied trans products in a chemically balanced pathway explanation. The authors' concerted electrophilic cyclization is the reference answer; scientifically equivalent physical explanations supported by actual evidence are acceptable. Merely repeating products or listing candidates is insufficient. Explain the relative trans configuration; a trans route alone cannot establish quantified trans/cis selectivity. A unique mechanism or complete catalytic cycle need not be demonstrated.

### r_energetic_comparison_answer

In the final conclusion, use actual barriers and net reaction thermodynamics with consistent physical references to judge that local sulfonamide cyclization is easier than that of the acid substrate and that both net product formations are exergonic, applying this comparison to the accessibility question for the supplied system. Passing independent numerical windows does not replace the ordering and signs; values outside the windows do not automatically invalidate evidence-supported qualitative conclusions. Do not treat full-profile heights as local barriers, ion-formation energies as activation barriers or the small 0.2 kcal/mol product difference as a robust ordering. Do not infer exact experimental rates/yields from this comparison.
