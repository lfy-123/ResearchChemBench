# Author-provided scientific guidance

The authors propose that basic conditions can produce neutral Mn(I) amido forms of the supplied ligand. Their A(R), named Mn1-R in the paper, has imidazole N3 and deprotonated amido N bound to Mn, three carbon-bound CO ligands and a pendant thioether. Their B(R), Mn2-R, has those N donors plus coordinated thioether S and two carbon-bound CO ligands. R is methyl or tert-butyl. Both have total charge zero and no initial N-H or Mn-H. Their amine-hydride counterparts have one N-H and one Mn-H. These are hypotheses for comparison, not measured solution populations. An optional sulfur-free NN tricarbonyl control is separate.

The baseline compares isopropanol dehydrogenation and acetophenone hydrogenation with these candidates. It studies a stepwise outer-sphere route with alcohol O-H and C-H transfer and, in the reverse half-reaction, Mn-H and N-H transfer. It also distinguishes the separated amido-plus-isopropanol reference from an N-protonated Mn-isopropoxide state when discussing effective barriers. Compare S/R substrate orientations for a specified catalyst configuration. Do not infer bulk ee from one conformation of an achiral-ligand system. The author inner-sphere sulfur-dissociation extension is optional, and no precursor activation barrier or population determination is required.

The method is Gaussian 16, M06L/all-electron TZVP, gas-phase unconstrained optimization without molecular truncation, analytical frequencies and IRC. Single-point energies use M06L/TZVP with GD3 and SMD isopropanol. Relative Gibbs energies are specified at 298.15 K and 1 atm. SI Table S2 includes gas-phase electronic/thermal contributions, solution totals and imaginary-frequency counts, while Table S3 supplies Cartesian coordinates. Construct starting geometries from the supplied complete molecular graphs, candidate coordination models and proposed hydrogen-transfer topology, then optimize and validate the relevant states.

The main figures and SI solution totals are not numerically self-consistent for every relative energy under the stated ledgers. Preserve the two source conventions when checking your model; they are not a reason to force an exact answer. The precise thermal assembly, source spin multiplicities, grids and original input decks remain incompletely specified. Document any chosen correction scheme and justified substitutions, retain gas and solution electronic terms separately, and define every particle ledger. Verify molecular and stereochemical identity rather than trusting a label alone.


## Private source limitations

- Main Figures 4–6 and SI Table S2 solution Gtot do not produce identical barriers using the explicitly stated separated-reactant ledgers. Example Mn1-tBu S/R barriers: main 10.63/11.67, direct SI arithmetic 9.14219/11.06738 kcal/mol. The corresponding gap is main 1.04 versus SI 1.92520. Mn2-tBu gap is main 2.02 versus SI 2.91039. All source sides and arithmetic are preserved in source_audit/energy_table_audit.json. Do not select one side as unique gold or enlarge tolerances.
- Some reaction thermodynamics or fine orderings change across sources: Mn1-tBu alcohol oxidation is −1.04 kcal/mol in the main text but +0.06777 from SI Gtot; Mn1 Me/tBu oxidation ordering and the tert-butyl R-channel NN/NNS reduction ordering are not source-robust. These are excluded from mandatory directional targets.
- The robust current scientific scope is matched candidate identities; NN tricarbonyl versus NNS dicarbonyl oxidation ordering; the S-channel reduction ordering; within a fixed handed configuration the S/R energetic asymmetry; and the coupled half-reaction account. Absolute barrier/ΔΔG targets remain source-qualified pending reconciliation, so there are no unique numerical-closeness rules for the conflicted quantities.
- SI Table S2 supplies gas E/ZPE/Hcorr/Gcorr/NImag and solution E/Htot/Gtot. Its solution Gtot values are not invariably identical to solution E plus the printed gas Gcorr. The exact correction assembly must be audited before attributing discrepancies to a method. Original input decks, grids, frequency scaling and multiplicity are not printed; a closed-shell singlet Mn(I) model is an explicit assumption unless supported by new evidence.
- The source label TS2(Mn-Me) is assigned to Mn2-Me by the surrounding Table S2 rows and composition; retain this alias rather than silently changing the source. Some later proton-transfer TS Gibbs ordinates are lower than adjacent intermediate ordinates; validate the electronic stationary points and thermochemical definitions.
- The selected achiral-ligand catalyst geometries can be chiral conformations/configurations. Their S/R differences and source 84%/96% estimates do not establish ensemble ee. Include mirror-related catalyst forms/populations only if making such an additional claim; bulk ee is not a gold.
