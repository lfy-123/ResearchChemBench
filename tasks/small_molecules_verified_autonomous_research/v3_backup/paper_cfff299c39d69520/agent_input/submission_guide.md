# Reproducible evidence submission

Use agent-defined IDs for models, operations, artifacts, quantitative results, claims and validations. Record complete molecular identities, charge and electronic state, relevant stereochemistry, method, solvent, temperature, standard state, stoichiometry and the reference defining each result. Link every quantitative claim to actual inputs, native outputs and an extraction location; preserve generated evidence under outputs/ and analysis scripts under code/.

Use complete, partial, bounded_failure or input_blocked truthfully. Partial and failed submissions may have empty result arrays when no physical result was obtained. Do not invent barriers, winners, endpoints or numerical values to fill the schema. For failed or blocked work, provide failure_report with the affected scope, cause and any available evidence; record attempts and surviving results. The schema checks representation, not scientific validity. Missing reference findings receive no corresponding scientific credit.

For claims about minima, saddles, states and mechanistic connections provide the evidence necessary to support those claims. For energy comparisons document a balanced common reference and distinguish electronic energy, enthalpy, free energy, local activation barriers and overall energy spans. Provide uncorrected terms and conversion formulas when conventions differ. Do not treat a scan maximum, a normal program exit or an unconnected imaginary mode as a validated reaction barrier.

Methods and exploration are your choices. No hypothesis count, named intermediate sequence, fixed grid or number of calculations is required. Explain decisions in report/report.md. Scientific credit is assigned only to supported final conclusions. Methods, execution and artifact completeness provide evidence for those conclusions and are not independent score items. Use the supplied public inputs, available computational tools and general scientific/software documentation.

Use Gibbs free energies in kcal/mol at298.15K with a consistent1atm thermochemical convention for the primary profile. Give all species and their stoichiometric coefficients in each profile reference, including base, acid/base products, leaving ions and ligand reservoirs. Catalyst formation from precatalyst, local activation barriers and finite-path energy heights have different references. State each balanced expression and identify the principal activation demand within the tested path. No catalyst-regeneration calculation, full-cycle energy span or steady-state turnover frequency is required. For temperature interpretation, distinguish a conditional constant-ΔG Eyring illustration from an actual temperature-dependent free-energy calculation; do not silently treat ΔG(298.15K) as a computed ΔG(398.15K).

## Final scientific conclusions

Address each required result explicitly in the final answer and connect it to the existing results/claims/artifacts records. Calculations and validation evidence support conclusions; their number or the order in which they were produced is not scored. Use the existing schema.
