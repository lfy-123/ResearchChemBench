## Connectivity verification and source-label reconciliation

Public quinoxalin-2(1H)-one implies the lactam N-H adjacent to C2=O, not substitution on the opposite pyridine-like nitrogen. Public methyl 2-diazo-2-phenylacetate implies a diazo-bearing alpha carbon attached to an unsubstituted phenyl group and a methyl ester. The ordinary reaction diagram agrees with both. The observed product name and SI page-15 graph put the new alpha-carbon/lactam-nitrogen bond at N1 while retaining C2=O. No source naming correction, locant reinterpretation or invented stereochemistry is needed.

The identity-only audit parsed exact coordinate evidence from SI pages 35, 36 and 37. Every audited record contains 17 carbon, 2 nitrogen and 3 oxygen heavy atoms and preserves the expected fused heterocycle, phenyl ring and ester/O-methyl connectivity. Source-coordinate N4 is the chemical lactam N1 adjacent to source C3/O12; source C29 is the phenyl/ester-bearing reacting carbon. In SI TS2-to-3, C29-N4 = 1.936 angstrom and C29-O12 = 2.986 angstrom. In SI TS2-to-4, C29-N4 = 3.067 angstrom and C29-O12 = 1.782 angstrom. The singlet SI Int3 has C29-N4 = 1.462 angstrom; SI Int4 instead has C29-O12 = 1.434 angstrom. These are reviewer-derived distances for identity checking only, not author-reported kinetic results.

This independently verifies the frozen contract's reconciliation: main Figure 2 calls the N branch TS2-to-4/Int4 and O branch TS2-to-3/Int3, whereas the SI table/coordinates use the reciprocal numbers. SI N-coupled coordinates are associated with the lower-energy table branch, and SI O-coupled coordinates with the higher-energy branch, consistent with the chemically identified main results. The discrepancy is systematic numbering, not contradictory computed chemistry requiring selection of a convenient molecular graph or a new reference value. Original reference records, the reconciliation and the thermal-label ambiguity remain private. No correction history or optimized coordinates enters autonomous inputs.
