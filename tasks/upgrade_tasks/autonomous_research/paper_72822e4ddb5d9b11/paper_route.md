# Author route and expanded benchmark scope

Main p4 and SI pp10–11 use B3LYP and BP86 with def2-TZVP on Ni and first-coordination-sphere atoms Cl1/Cl2/N1/N2/C1/C2 and def2-SVP elsewhere. The authors favor a closed-shell solution and report open-shell starts collapsing to it; their triplet comparison is an electronic-energy comparison. Toluene SMD used for optical calculations is a separate condition. The benchmark requires explicit independent BS, wavefunction stability and vertical/relaxed controls.

## Expanded development scope

Determine whether the full Ni complex supports a closed-shell description, a triplet or a distinct broken-symmetry state, and whether the conclusion survives geometry and electronic-method controls.
