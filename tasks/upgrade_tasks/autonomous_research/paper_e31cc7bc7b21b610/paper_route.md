# Author route and expanded benchmark scope

Main pp7–9/Figures5–7 attributes a short Ir–Ir bond partly to ligand-assisted pi interactions. Source Gaussian B3LYP calculations use Ir SDD and 6-31G* light-atom basis and simplified Hap=1,2-C6H4(NH)O; the source (Hap)4Ir2 S4 structure is a minimum while constrained C2h is a first-order saddle. Full Egan2Ir2 is experimentally characterized. The benchmark Egan-to-egan paired tert-butyl truncation, neutral-doublet fragment energies and distance/twist interventions are new; Hap simplification cannot validate their values.

## Expanded development scope

Determine whether metal–ligand orbital mixing is needed to explain the Ir–Ir bonding response, using full and exactly truncated dimers, fixed fragment definitions and distance/twist interventions.
