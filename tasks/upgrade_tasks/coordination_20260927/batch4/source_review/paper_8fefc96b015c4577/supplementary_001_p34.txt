8. Computational Details:
All geometries under investigation were fully optimized using the dispersion-corrected B3LYP-D3 functional5

in combination with the def2-TZVPP basis set.6 Solvent effects were modeled using the SMD solvation model7,

with dimethyl sulfoxide as the solvent for the hydroquinoline 3a pathway and 1,2-dichloroethane for the

benzosultam 5a pathway. Vibrational frequency analyses were then performed at the same level of theory to

confirm the nature of each stationary point—minima (no imaginary frequencies) or transition states (one

imaginary frequency). All computations were carried out with Gaussian 16.8
