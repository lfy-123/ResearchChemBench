                   1. In this work, we developed a bifunctional ionic liquid catalyst for the oxidative carboxylation of olefins under mild conditions. As a result, cyclic carbonatesPublished
              can be directly synthesized from low-cost olefins instead of epoxides, avoiding the intensive energy consumption and waste formation in the traditional epoxi-
                dation process.
                   2. The organocatalyst system in this work does not contain any transition metal and thus prevents heavy metal contamination. Moreover, it enables the use
                 of aqueous hydrogen peroxide as a cost-eﬀective and green oxidant while eﬀectively inhibiting the formation of byproducts from intermediate hydrolysis.
                   3. Future research will be dedicated to catalyst immobilization, thereby facilitating the product separation and catalyst recycle, expediting the potential appli-
                cation of the one-step synthesis of cyclic carbonates directly from olefins.
