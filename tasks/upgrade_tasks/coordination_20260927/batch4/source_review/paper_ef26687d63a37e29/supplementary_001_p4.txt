Generation of model compounds by (Et2N)3P and CA for epoxide activation studies

    Through the above pathway, (Et2N)3P and CA interacted to prepare model

compounds, using (Et2N)3P and PA as examples. Specifically. (Et2N)3P (23.5 μL, 0.086

mmol), PA (12.74 mg, 0.086 mmol) and 0.1 mL C6D6 (as solvent) were sequentially

added to the sample vial, and the reaction was allowed to proceed for 15 min at room

temperature. Subsequently, TFPO (7.0 μL, 0.086 mmol) was added to the mixture. After

the reaction, 0.4 mL of C6D6 was added to the sample vial, and then the mixture was

transferred to the NMR tube, which was sealed and then transferred out of the glovebox

for NMR analysis.

Generation of model compounds by (Et2N)3P and PA for sodium ethoxide

activation studies.

   A 10 mL sample vial was prepared by drying it in an oven at 110°C for more than

4 h and then immediately transferring it to a glovebox filled with an argon atmosphere.

Firstly, (Et2N)3P (10.9 μL, 0.04 mmol), PA (5.92 mg, 0.04 mmol) and 50 μL THF (as

solvent) were sequentially added to the sample vial, and the reaction was allowed to

proceed for 15 min at room temperature. The model compounds obtained from the

preparation were divided into two equal molar amounts of the product, one portion was

directly dissolved in 0.6 mL of CDCl3, transferred to an NMR tube, and sealed for NMR

analysis. The other portion was treated with an equimolar amount of sodium ethoxide

(1.36 mg, 0.02 mmol), dissolved in 0.6 mL of CDCl3, transferred to an NMR tube, and

sealed for NMR analysis.
