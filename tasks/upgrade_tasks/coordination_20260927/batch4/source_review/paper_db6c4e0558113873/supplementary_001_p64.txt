5.11    Light on-off experiments (syq-6-167)





    To a Schlenk tube (100 mL) were added 4CzIPN (8.2 mg, 0.01 mmol) and Cu(acac)2

(26.6 mg, 0.1 mmol) sequentially. The Schlenk tube was then degassed and refilled with N2

for three times. Then 6b (231.1 mg, 1.0 mmol)/CH3CN (20 mL) and 2a (358.2 mg, 2.0 mmol)

were added sequentially under N2. The resulting mixture was irradiated with blue LEDs (427

nm, 40 W, and about 5 cm away) at 40 oC of oil bath. The crude product was analyzed by 1H

NMR spectrum with mesitylene as an internal standard. The reaction mixture was irradiated

with blue LEDs and stirred for 1 hour, then the reaction mixture was stirred for 1 hour in dark.

This process was repeated for another two cycles to afford the light on-off plot.
