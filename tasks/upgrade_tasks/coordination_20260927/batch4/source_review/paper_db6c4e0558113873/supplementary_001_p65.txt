6. DFT Computational Studies

Computational Methodology

    All calculations were performed with the Gaussian 16 packages.19 Geometries have been

fully optimized with the density functional theory of B3LYP method,20 including Grimme’s

D3   dispersion   corrections  (B3LYP-D3   with  Becke-Johnson   damping).21,22  The

LANL2DZ23-25 basis set in conjunction with the LANL2DZ pseudopotential26 was used for

Cu atom, and the 6-31G (d,p) basis set27,28 was used for the other atoms. Harmonic vibration

frequency calculations were conducted on all the stationary points, which confirmed each as

an energy minimum (no imaginary frequency). Besides, the single-point energy calculations

for the optimized geometry  in  acetonitrile  (ε = 35.7) were performed using M0629,30

functional with SDD31 basis set for Cu and 6-311+G(d,p) basis set for the other atoms in

SMD32 solvation model. The reported energies are the solution-phase Gibbs free energies

(ΔGsol) in acetonitrile. The 3D diagrams of molecules were generated using CYLview.33
