5.  Mechanistic studies

5.1 The reaction of 6b with 2a in the absence of 4CzIPN (syq-6-171-B)





   A dried Schlenk tube (25 mL) containing Cu(acac)2 (2.6 mg, 0.01 mmol) was evacuated

and backfilled with nitrogen for three times. Then 6b (23.6 mg, 0.1 mmol)/CH3CN (2 mL)

and 2a (35.8 mg, 0.2 mmol) were added sequentially. The resulting mixture was irradiated

with blue LEDs (427 nm, 40 W, and about 5 cm away) at 40 oC in an oil bath for 16 hours as

monitored by TLC and filtered through a short column of silica gel eluted with ethyl acetate

(15 mL × 3). After concentration, the crude product was analyzed by 1H NMR spectrum with

CH2Br2 as an internal standard: No signal of the corresponding products was found and 97%

recovery of 6b was observed.
