 Flow microreactor was consisted of three pre-cooling units (P1, P2 and P3; inner diameter φ = 1000

μm), two micromixers (R1 and R2), and two microreactors (M1 and M2). SM (0.050 M; flow rate = 10

mL/min) was pushed into M1 and mixed LiNp (0.30 M; flow rate = 5.0 mL/min) in M1, and reacted

in R1. Then the mixture was quenched by MeOH (0.30 M; flow rate = 5.0 mL/min) in M2, and the

resulting solution was quenched completely by saturated NH4Cl aq as external quench. After the steady

state (30 seconds), the resulting solution was taken for 30 seconds, and undecane (30 μL) was added

to the solution as an internal standard. The yields were determined by gas chromatography.
