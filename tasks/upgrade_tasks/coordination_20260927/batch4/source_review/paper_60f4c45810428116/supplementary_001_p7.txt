2-Methylbenzenemethanethiol (3a)





α-bromo-o-xylene (1.95g, 10.5 mmol, 1.0 equiv), thioacetic acid (0.913 g, 12.2 mmol, 1.2 equiv),

potassium carbonate (3.06 g, 22.1 mmol, 2.0 equiv) and THF (30 mL) were added into a 200 mL

round-bottomed flask. After stirring for 30 min at rt, MeOH (30 mL) was added and the solution was

stirred for another 30 min at rt. After quenching by 1.0 M HCl (4.0 mL), the mixture was evaporated

to remove THF and MeOH. Then the water layer was extracted by CHCl3 (30 mL) 3 times and washed

with brine once. The organic layer was dried over Na2SO4, filtered and concentrated. The crude was

purified by silica-gel column chromatography (eluent; hexane/AcOEt = 95:5) to obtain compound A

1.58 g (83%) as bright yellow oil.1 The compound was determined by 1H and 13C NMR spectroscopy

and then used for next reaction.
