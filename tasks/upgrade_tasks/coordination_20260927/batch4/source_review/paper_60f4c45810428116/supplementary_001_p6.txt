We settled a two-dimensional grid plot with temperature T (°C) and residence time t1 (s) of the
active intermediate as explanatory variables and performed a spatial search to determine the
optimal conditions with the yield z of the product as a target variable (Figure S 1). Based on five
reactors with different volumes, we varied the residence time while keeping the flow rate 15.0
mL/min constant during the active species generation phase at reactor R1, conducted reaction
trials at five temperature ranges, and obtained a total of 23 gridded data points except for two
missing data points due to the clogging of flow channels. Interpolation of this grid data with
extrapolation of missing points was performed to create a contour plot of reaction yields for target
product 2a and byproduct 3a.
