1. General information
1H NMR spectra were measured on JEOL 400 MHz FT-400 NMR. Mass spectra
were measured on a MALDI-TOF Mass spectrometer. The UV-Vis absorption spectra
were recorded with a TU-1901 UV-Vis spectrophotometer. The fluorescence
luminescence
spectra
were
measured
using
a
LS55
type
fluorescence
spectrophotometer from PerkinElmer. Transient PL curves were recorded on a horiba
Fluorolog-QM and excited with a 365nm laser light source. Thermogravimetric
analysis (TGA) was performed using a USA Waters Q600 under a nitrogen
atmosphere at a heating rate of 10 °C min−1. Differential scanning calorimetry (DSC)
was performed using a METTLER TOLEDO DSC instrument at a heating rate of
10 °C min−1 under a nitrogen atmosphere. Cyclic voltammetry (CV) measurement
was conducted with an RST 3100 electrochemical work station, with ferrocene
(Fc+/Fc) as an internal standard. The electrode configuration consists of an Ag/AgNO3
reference electrode, a Pt wire counter electrode and glassy a carbon working electrode.
The CV scan was conducted using a tetra-n-butylammoniumhexafluorophosphate
(Bu4NPF6) acetonitrile solution (0.1 M) as an electrolyte. Molecular simulation
calculations are performed at B3LYP/6-31G (d, p) by Gaussian 16 program and
viewed through GaussView 6.0 software.
2. Device fabrication
The multilayer OLEDs were fabricated by the vacuum-deposition method. ITO
obtained from commercial channels but requires further processing. Ultrasonic treat
ITO with cleaning agent, deionized water, acetone, and isopropanol solvents for 15
minutes, respectively, and then dry at 100 ° C for 2 hours. NPB, TCTA, TPBi, LiF,
and Al are also obtained from commercial sources but do not require further
processing. An-σ-Ph and An-σ-DA were purified by vacuum temperature gradient
sublimation. The cleaned ITO is treated with ozone for 15 minutes, and then

transferred to the evaporation chamber. In a high vacuum environment (1×10-5 Pa),
the functional layer materials, luminescent materials, and Al are sequentially
deposited at high temperatures. When evaporating and depositing organic material
thin films, the evaporation rate is controlled at 1-2 Å s-1; When evaporating LiF, the
evaporation rate is controlled at 0.1 Å s-1; When evaporating Al, the evaporation rate
is controlled at 3 Å s-1, and 9 mm2 of the luminescent area. All the OLEDs were
measured at room temperature in the dark. The Current Density-Voltage-Luminance
(J-V-L) characteristics were measured with a Keithley 2400 source meter and a LS110
luminance meter. The electroluminescence (EL) spectra were collected using a
spectrascan
PR650
spectrophotometer.
The
EQEs
were
calculated
from
the
Luminance-Current density characteristics and EL spectra with the hypothesis of
Lambertian distribution. The transient EL decay was tested by an Agilent 8114A
pulse generator to generate rectangular pulse voltages.
