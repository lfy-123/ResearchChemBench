Photomasking: A Si wafer was coated with a layer of Apiezon type N grease, which was pressed
grease side down onto a sample of 1 to coat the wafer. Excess MOF was removed by gently tapping
the wafer on edge. The wafer was placed coating side up on a glass slide, and a laminated paper
photomask was applied to the MOF coating and secured with tape. The wafer was brought into a
N2 drybox and placed in a Pyrex dish. A fine layer of silicone grease was applied to the edge of
the dish and a quartz window was pressed onto the grease to seal the sample under N2. The dish
was removed from the drybox and irradiated with 254 nm light in a Fisher Scientific 175 W UV
crosslinker for 3 h. The sample was removed from the dish and the photomask was gently removed
to reveal the masked image.

utilized the atom-pairwise dispersion correction with the Becke-Johnson damping scheme

For the calculations of singlet-triplet (S-T) gaps, the same functional and basis set were employed
with def2-svp-jkfit auxiliary basis function in PySCF. The multi-collinear SF-TDDFT11, 12 is
implemented and available in PySCF-forge.13, 14 The SF-TDDFT reference state is the spin-
unrestricted DFT calculations of Sz=1 component triplet states. The singlet and triplet states were
obtained by flipping spin-up to spin-down electrons. The S-T gap was obtained as the spin-flip
energy difference between singlet and triplet states. We note that for conventional collinear SF-
TDDFT, the spin-flip triplet and the reference triplet states are not degenerate. The more rigorous
multi-collinear SF-TDDFT (without Tamm-Dancoff Approximation, TDA) removes this non-
physical degeneracy breaking.

Synthesis of anthracene/9,10-anthracenedicarboxylic acid heterodimer (diAn(COOH)2). A 125 mL
Pyrex  Erlenmeyer  flask was  charged  with  anthracene  (0.356  g,  2 mmol)  and  9,10-
anthracenedicarboxylic acid (0.500 g, 1.88 mmol), a stir bar, and 70 mL of tetrahydrofuran. The
solution was sealed with a rubber septum and electrical tape and sparged with N2 for 60 minutes.
The yellow solution was irradiated with 365 nm light while vigorously stirring for 24 h, at which
point the solution had turned colorless with a fine colorless precipitate. The solution was vacuum
filtered through a Buchner funnel and the precipitate was washed three times with 10 mL of THF.
The filtrate solvent was removed under vacuum to produce a beige, microcrystalline powder
identified as the THF solvate of diAn(COOH)2 (1.57 THF per dimer as determined by 1H NMR after
exposure to vacuum for 60 h).

Benchmark calculations of single-triplet energy gaps
A comparison between calculated and experimentally measured singlet-triplet gaps is given in
Table S4. The calculated values agree well with the experimental measurement, both in absolute
value and the relative trend. To assess the accuracy of calculated S-T gaps, we performed
benchmark calculations for the functional dependence, basis-set effects, and the dependence on
equilibrium structures. All the calculations in this subsection were performed for the pyridine
(R=H) variant. The calculated S-T gaps using SF-TDA with B3LYP, PBE015, and CAM-B3LYP16
functionals are 335, 316, and 308 cm-1, respectively. The three hybrid functionals reasonably agree
with each other. However, SF-TDA with PBE functional17 overestimate the S-T gap to 609 cm-1,
demonstrating the importance of including exact Hartree-Fock exchange. The SF-TDDFT with
B3LYP functional gives a similar prediction on S-T gap as 344 cm-1 to that from SF-TDA, the
difference between which is negligible in this context. To test the basis-set convergence, we used
def2-TZVP basis set for Cu atoms. The calculated S-T gap with triple-zeta basis set is 343 cm-1,
showing negligible basis-set effects. We further optimized the equilibrium structure using minimal
