I . Experimental 
Characterization and measurements.
The  1 H NMR and  13 C NMR spectra were performed on the Bruker Ascend 400 MHz spectrometer. High-resolution mass spectra were detected with ThermoScientificTM Q-Exactive. UV-Vis optical absorption spectra were measured on a Shimadzu UV-3600 UV-VIS-NIR spectrophotometer. Cyclic voltammetry measurements were carried out under argon atmosphere using the CHI760E voltammetric workstation with a N 2 -saturated solution of 0.1 M tetra-n-butyl ammonium hexafluoro phosphate (Bu 4 NPF 6 ) in CH 2 Cl 2  as the supporting electrolyte. A platinum disk working electrode, a platinum wire counter electrode, and a silver wire reference electrode were employed, and the ferrocene/ferrocenium redox couple (Fc/Fc + ) was used as the reference for all measurements with a scanning rate of 50 mV s − 1 . Thermogravimetric (TGA) measurements were carried out on a METTLER TOLEDO (TGA 1 STARe System) apparatus at a heat ramp of 10 °C min − 1  under N 2 . DSC curves were recorded on a differential scanning calorimetry in nitrogen (Mettler, STARe, heating ramp: 10 °C min − 1 ).   T he Photoluminescence (PL) spectra were characterized by an Agilent fluorescence spectrophotometer at room temperature.
Theoretical calculations.
The geometry optimization was performed by DFT method at the B3LYP/6-31(d) level .  All the calculations were carried out using Gaussian 09 program package.
Device Fabrication.
Indium tin oxide (ITO) conductive glass was used as the substrate to fabricate OLED devices with an area of 3×3 mm 2 . The ITO substrates were cleaned with detergent powder and deionized water, ultrasonicated in acetone and isopropanol for 10 minutes, and then treated with ultraviolet lamp ozone for 30 minutes. Then, the ITO substrate was loaded into a vacuum chamber with a vacuum degree of less than 5×10 −4  Pa, and according to the designed device structure, the organic functional layers were deposited sequentially according to different thicknesses, the organic layer was deposited at a rate of 1.0 Å s −1 , the molybdenum trioxide (MoO 3 ) and lithium fluoride (LiF) were deposited at a rate of 0.1 Å s −1 , and the aluminum (Al) electrode was deposited at a rate of 5.0 Å s −1  to obtain a complete OLED device. Electroluminescence spectra and CIE color coordinates were measured using a PR-655 spectrophotometer; The Keithley-2400 Source Meter and ST-900M Spot Brightness Meter were used to measure the voltage (V), luminance (L), current density (J), current efficiency (CE), and power efficiency (PE) of the device. The external quantum efficiency (EQE) of the device is calculated by the above parameters.
II.   Synthesis 
5-(4-(9H-carbazol-9-yl)phenyl)pyrazine-2,3-diamine (1)

Compounds  5-bromopyrazine-2,3-diamine   (1 .00  g, 5.29 mmol),   (4-(9H-carbazol-9-yl)phenyl)boronic acid  (1.67 g, 5.82 mmol) and bistriphenylphosphine palladium dichloride ( 0.02  g, 0.0175 mmol) were added to a 100 mL three-mouth bottle in a nitrogen atmosphere, potassium carbonate aqueous solution (2M, 10 mL), toluene (6 mL) and ethanol (3 mL) were added to the reaction system, and heated to reflux reaction under magnetic stirring for 4 hours. After the reaction solution was cooled, 500 mL of water was added to quench the reaction, and the organic phase was extracted with dichloromethane (100 mL), and then filtered and dried with anhydrous MgSO 4 . After solvent removal under reduced pressure, the mixture was purified by column chromatography with dichloromethane/n-hexane = 1/1 as the eluent. The resulting product was recrystallized in a toluene/ethanol mixture solvent to obtain brown powder  1  (1.25 g, 67%). 1 H NMR (400 MHz, DMSO- d 6 ) δ 8.25 (s, 2H), 8.14 (d,  J  = 8.5 Hz, 2H), 7.93 (s, 1H), 7.62 (d,  J  = 8.5 Hz, 2H), 7.44 (s, 4H), 7.30 (ddd,  J  = 7.9, 5.4, 2.6 Hz, 2H), 6.15 (d,  J  = 19.0 Hz, 4H).

After the reaction solution was cooled, 500 mL of water was added to quench the reaction, and the organic phase was extracted with dichloromethane (100 mL), and then filtered and dried with anhydrous MgSO4. After solvent removal under reduced pressure, the mixture was purified by column chromatography with dichloromethane/n-hexane = 1/1 as the eluent.
