# Private paper route

## 1. Scientific objective and author claim

The paper uses quantum-chemical optimized structures to explain concentration-dependent packing of the naphthalene-derived dication G1. The authors claim that an isolated G1 geometry is twisted, while a G1 dimer adopts offset π-stacking identified as a J-aggregate; these computed geometries support the observed emission changes.

## 2. System and model boundary

The computed systems are the G1 monomer and an overall +4 dimer assembly of two identical G1 dications (with implicit counterions omitted in the reported coordinates). The reported calculations use an aqueous dielectric continuum and ground-state electronic structures. Structural observables are the benzyl–pyridinium and pyridinium–naphthalene dihedrals for the monomer, and naphthalene-ring centroid separation and slip angle for the dimer.

## 3. Authors' implemented computational route

| Step | Purpose | Input | Method/software | Key parameters | Output | Source evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Optimize isolated G1 | SI optimized-monomer coordinates | DFT geometry optimization, Gaussian 16 | B3LYP-D3/6-31G(d), PCM water, ground state | optimized monomer and vibrational minimum | ev_doc_005e9539d38b_000082_ecb1b30edde8; ev_doc_3bbc541115e8_000028_3741e3bced78 |
| 2 | Optimize stacked G1 dimer | SI optimized-dimer coordinates | DFT geometry optimization, Gaussian 16 | B3LYP-D3/6-31G(d), PCM water, ground state | optimized dimer and vibrational minimum | ev_doc_005e9539d38b_000082_ecb1b30edde8; ev_doc_3bbc541115e8_000029_5baca7b95a41 |
| 3 | Relate geometry to packing assignment | outputs of steps 1–2 | geometric analysis of optimized structures | ring dihedrals, ring centroids, interplanar/slip geometry | structural parameters supporting twisted monomer and J-like dimer | ev_doc_005e9539d38b_000169_da026dcba44a |

## 4. Validation and analysis protocol

The authors checked that optimized structures had no imaginary frequencies, treating this as evidence of energetic minima. They measured the specified dihedrals and dimer packing geometry and interpreted the offset parallel arrangement as J-aggregate-like packing. The paper also compares the computed motifs with NMR-derived host–guest/aggregation interpretations.

## 5. Private reference results

For G1, the reported benzyl–pyridinium dihedrals are 50.67° and 50.65°, and the pyridinium–naphthalene dihedrals are 30.28° and 30.27°. For the G1 dimer, the reported interplanar stacking separation is 3.63 Å (the later paragraph gives the full naphthalene-centroid distance as 5.09 Å) and slip angle is 44.89°. The paper states that no imaginary frequencies were found.

## 6. Limitations and interpretation boundaries

The SI coordinates are optimized author endpoints and are evaluator-private references for this geometry-discovery task. Public inputs now use independent unoptimized chemical-graph embeddings, not those endpoint coordinates. The paper's structural assignment is qualitative and does not by itself prove bulk aggregation thermodynamics. Counterions and explicit solvent are not part of the supplied coordinate boundary. Dihedral sign conventions and ring-plane/centroid definitions must be stated by the investigator.
