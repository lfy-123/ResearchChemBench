2   Photophysical Properties

   The  effect of organic compounds on  optical  properties  in  different solvent

environments can be described by the Lippert-Mataga equation:


                                                         2
          ℎ𝑐(𝑣𝑎−𝑣𝑓) = ℎ𝑐(𝑣𝑎0 −𝑣𝑓𝑜) −2(𝜇𝑒−𝜇𝑔) 𝑓（ɛ，n）                                          𝑎3

where ƒ is the orientational polarizability of the solvent. 𝑣𝑎0 −𝑣𝑐𝑜 corresponds to the

Stokes shifts when ƒ is zero. μe is the excited-state dipole moment, derived from the

Avogadro number (N), molecular weight (M), and density (d). μg is the ground-state

dipole moment, a is the solvent Onsager cavity radius, and ε and n are the solvent

dielectric and the solvent refractive index, respectively. ƒ (ε, n) and a can be calculated

respectively as follows:

                              Ɛ −1    −1                   𝑓(ɛ，n) =    −𝑛2
                             2Ɛ + 1  2𝑛2 + 1

                                                3√3𝑀                  𝑎=
                            4𝜋𝑁𝑑

The detailed data were listed in Table S4.
