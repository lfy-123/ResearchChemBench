                     Description of Additional Supplementary Files



File Name: Supplementary Movie 1

Description: Time- and spatially resolved NEXAFS imaging using secondary electrons
generated under X-ray illumination with photon energies ranging from 850.4 to 868.6 eV,
capturing the growth of a γ′-Ni3Al precipitate island during UHV annealing of a βNiAl(100)
single crystal at 800 ºC.



File Name: Supplementary Data 1

Description: Zipped file containing structure models used to generate calculated data shown in
Figs. 3, 4 and 6.
