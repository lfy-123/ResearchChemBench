2.3. Measurements
   1H NMR and 1C NMR were measured on Bruker-Advance 400 MHz
NMR spectrometer and Agilent 600 MHz DD2. UV/Vis experiments were
carried out with a TU-1810 instrument with 1 cm pathlength cells at
298 K. Fluorescence spectra were measured in a conventional rectan­
gular quartz cell (10 × 10 × 45 mm) on a F-4600 spectrometer. To verify
the feasibility of different arrangements of the host-guest complexes,
DFT calculations were performed at the B3LYP-D3/6-31G(d) level of
theory using the Gaussian 16 software. Additionally, a polarizable
continuum solvent model (PCM) was used throughout the calculations
to represent the standard water dielectric medium. No imaginary fre­
quencies were found, which confirms achieving the energetic minima.
