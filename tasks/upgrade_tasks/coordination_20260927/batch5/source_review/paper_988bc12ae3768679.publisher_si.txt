Details on the 1H-NMR Spectra Simulation
The 1H-NMR chemical shifts were calculated at the CAM-B3LYP/6-31+G(d) level of theory using the optimized geometries obtained at the same level of theory. All calculations were performed using DMSO as solvent within the Polarizable Continuum Model (PCM) framework to account for solvation effects. Relevant Cartesian coordinates are provided below. The NMR shielding tensors were computed using the Gauge-Independent Atomic Orbital (GIAO) method to ensure gauge invariance of the calculated magnetic properties. Chemical shifts were referenced to tetramethylsilane (TMS) using the standard formula δ = σ(TMS) - σ(compound), where σ represents the calculated isotropic shielding constant. The chemical shifts of the aromatic hydrogens adjacent to the hydroxyl groups, which are discussed in the main text and show significant pH-dependent variations, are summarized in Table S1. All calculations were performed with Gaussian 16 (revision B.01) package.
