Keywords:                                           Traditional electrolytes for supercapacitors, typically employing single-charged ions like TEA+ in 1 M tetrae­
Divalent cation                              thylammonium tetrafluoroborate/propylene carbonate (TEA-BF4/PC), suffer from thick, loose solvation shells.
Solvent modification                               This structure impedes ion adsorption at the electrode interface and causes excessive solvent molecules (PC) to
Multicharge adsorption                                          decompose and generate gas under high voltage, severely limiting both operating voltage and energy storageHigh voltage                                                       efficiency. To address this, we introduced the divalent salt N,N-1,4-diethyl-trivinyl diamine tetrafluoroborate
                                            (DEDABCO-(BF4)2) into the TEA-BF4/PC electrolyte. The presence of the divalent DEDABCO2+ cation facilitates
                                                 the desolvation of TEA+ ions. This charge-driven reorganization at the interface reduces the population of PC
                                                   solvent molecules while simultaneously enhancing the interfacial charge density. Consequently, the cell achieves
                                             a higher operating voltage and improved specific capacity. Electrochemical testing shows a capacitor using this 1
                          M TEA-BF4/PC + 0.2 M DEDABCO-(BF4)2 hybrid electrolyte achieves a stable 3.4 V operating voltage and a high
                                                 discharge capacity of 122 F g−1. Additionally, the device delivers an excellent energy density of 50.4 Wh kg−1
                                          and a maximum power density of 8.9 kW kg−1. The resulting gains in stability and efficiency establish this dual-
                                                           salt electrolyte as a key enabler for next generation high energy density supercapacitors.
