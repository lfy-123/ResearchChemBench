Figure S26: Comparison of the signal shifts of the two different triazole protons (upper and lower curves) of AB-mTTA-Cy
 (c = 1 mM in Ac-d6) with TBACl for the major isomeric component (up triangles) and the minor component (down triangles).
Black lines represent the fit for the major component and red lines the fitted curve for the minor component.

At low salt concentration (equivalents), it can be observed that the Z isomer exhibits a slightly stronger shift if present as the
minor component, whereas the E isomer shows a slightly lower shift if present as the minor component. This can be explained
with the higher affinity of the Z Isomer towards the anion. At high salt concentrations no difference is visible. The resulting anion
binding constant of the minor component deviates around 10 % from the anion binding constant in the major case, remaining
 within the error margin. Therefore, our anion binding constants can be considered reliable.

2. DFT

Geometry optimizations and minimum energy path (MEP) calculations were executed with the Gaussian
16 Rev. B.01 package1 employing the B3LYP hybrid functional2 in conjunction with the 6-31G* basis
set.3 The standard convergence criteria for geometry optimizations and single point calculations were
used, along with the addition of empirical dispersion correction of type Grimme D3 with Becke-Johnson
damping.4–6 To include solvent effects into the calculations, the polarizable continuum model (PCM)7 for
acetone was used and the cavity for the molecule was formed based on the UFF model for atomic radii.8
For the visualization of molecular geometries, VMD 1.9.3 with the internal Tachyon renderer was used.9,10
Gibbs free energies were obtained accordingly by including zero point energy and thermodynamic
corrections  resulting from  additional harmonic  frequency  calculations  for  standard  temperature
T = 298.15 K. The anion binding constant was calculated according to the relation

where R denotes the universal gas constant and ΔG is calculated taking into account relaxed host
structures and counterpoise correction for compensating the basis set superposition error (BSSE).
