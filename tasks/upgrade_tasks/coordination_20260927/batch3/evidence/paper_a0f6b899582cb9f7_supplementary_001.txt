Scheme S1. Synthetic routes to M3.


Synthesis of compound M3: In a dried flask, tributyl(thiophen-2-yl)stannane (3.69 g, 11.34

mmol), 2,5-Dibromopyrazine (1 g, 4.2 mmol), and a catalytic amount of Pd(PPh3)4 were

dissolved in anhydrous degassed toluene (30 mL). The mixture was stirred at 110 °C for 24 h

under an argon atmosphere. Then the mixture was cooled to room temperature and was poured

into water and extracted with chloroform. The organic layer was dried over anhydrous MgSO4.

The solvents were removed by vacuum evaporation, and the resulting mixture was purified by

silica gel chromatography (Dichloromethane: Petroleum ether=2:1) to obtain M3 as a yellow

solid (810.3 mg, 79 %). 1H NMR (600 MHz, CDCl3): δ 8.87 (s, 2H), 7.71 - 7.64 (m, 2H), 7.47

(d, J = 5.0 Hz, 2H), 7.16 (t, J = 4.3 Hz, 2H).
