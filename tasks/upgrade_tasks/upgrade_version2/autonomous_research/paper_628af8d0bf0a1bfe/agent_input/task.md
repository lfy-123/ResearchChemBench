Develop and test an interpretation of any difference, or establish a defensible limit on what can be distinguished, and assess what your evidence supports about antiaromatic character within this pair. Neither a response ordering nor a substituent mechanism is given.

## Supplied facts and scope

`data/inputs/systems.json` specifies the full constitutional identities and source ground-state charge/spin. Focus on the magnetic response of the central pentalene unit in these isolated molecules. Choose and define the response measure, geometrical representation and evidential comparisons. Crystal packing, excited states, synthetic accessibility and material/device performance are outside the requested conclusion.
