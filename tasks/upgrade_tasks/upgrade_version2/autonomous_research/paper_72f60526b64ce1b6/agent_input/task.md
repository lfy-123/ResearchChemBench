# Scientific objective

Determine whether and under what conditions the bridge changes rectification within the supplied Group A molecules in Au(111) junctions. Develop and test a molecular-level explanation for the response, and establish how far the evidence supports it within the stated junction and bias domain.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

data/inputs/systems.json provides the three full Group A molecular graphs, atom maps, neutral isolated states and Au-bound fragment stoichiometry. These molecular identities define the research domain, not an expected ordering or list of explanatory mechanisms. Junctions use Au(111) electrodes and sulfur contacts with the stated source terminal-H removal; record any representation and its physical scope. Study only the source bias interval -2 to +2 V. Choose the bias sampling and temperature appropriate to your declared model and report them. Do not infer bulk-device efficiency or Group B behavior from this family. An isolated molecular response alone does not establish current through an open junction.

resource_limits.json describes the available command interface and remaining feasibility limits. The siesta/tbtrans5.4.2 command declaration is available, and prior developer evidence supports matched electrode calculations, but no full source-junction finite-bias reference is certified here. Source author QuantumATK availability is not asserted. Do not equate executable success or converged bare electrodes with a validated device. Private source PDFs/SI, optimized endpoints, verification artifacts, evaluation and other workspaces are not authorized AR inputs.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Choose a tractable investigation of bridge-dependent rectification in the supplied family. Justify why your objects and calculations are comparable and why their information can test your proposed explanation. Preserve the electrode/central-region, atom-ordering and energy-reference dependencies that make a transport result reproducible. Distinguish an electronic-structure descriptor from evidence of finite-bias conduction. Quantify the resolution and limitations of the chosen evidence; explain any response that is not distinguishable at that resolution. AR investigators choose the hypotheses, model, comparisons, numerical validation and stopping or revision decisions. PR investigators address the disclosed author baseline or justify substitutions, then independently design additional research. No added benchmark device variants, bias grid, orbital interpretation or rectification convention is required solely because it appeared in a private reference.

AR investigators select and revise their own hypotheses or models, methods, comparisons and decision criteria. PR investigators must reproduce the disclosed author baseline, or justify scientifically supported substitutions and explain their comparability; they design any additional investigation independently. In both modes evidence and scientific validity must be proportionate to the claims. The common scientific-result standard does not require a prescribed hypothesis count, a private reference matrix or agreement with the author answer.
