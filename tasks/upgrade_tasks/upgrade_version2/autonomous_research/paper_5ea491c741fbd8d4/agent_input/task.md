Establish which interpretations are supported, contradicted or unresolved within the authenticated crystal and the limits of the chosen evidence.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Develop an evidence-based account of the intermolecular interactions relevant to this crystal. Explain why your chosen representation and observations can test the claims you make, and account for the information lost by approximations, sampling or unavailable data. Distinguish geometric proximity from energetic or causal assertions. Report reproducible quantitative support and uncertainty for substantive claims. A surface analysis, electronic calculation or alternative physically justified route is assessed by what it establishes; for AR and additional PR investigation, no specific ranking, contact family, neighbor cutoff, decomposition or computational sequence is prescribed. PR must address the disclosed source baseline where its inputs permit.

AR investigators select and revise their own hypotheses or models, methods, comparisons and decision criteria. PR investigators must reproduce the disclosed author baseline, or justify scientifically supported substitutions and explain their comparability; they design any additional investigation independently. In both modes evidence and scientific validity must be proportionate to the claims. The common scientific-result standard does not require a prescribed hypothesis count, a private reference matrix or agreement with the author answer.
