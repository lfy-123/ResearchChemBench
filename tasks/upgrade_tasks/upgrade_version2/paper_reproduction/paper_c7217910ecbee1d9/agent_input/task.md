# Scientific objective

Determine whether and how B(C6F5)3 ligation changes electron acceptance by gas-phase PAl12 within the n = 0, 1 and 2 composition family. Develop and test an explanation consistent with the structures and electronic states actually investigated, and establish the scope and resolution of the resulting conclusions.

# Author-provided scientific guidance

The authors propose that boron Lewis-acid ligation shifts cluster electronic levels downward through local charge rearrangement while retaining superatomic states, increasing electron acceptance without changing the parent shell filling. For the source PAl12 series they report AEA values of approximately 1.76, 3.22 and 3.93 eV for zero, one and two B(C6F5)3 ligands (main pp2–4, Figs1–4). These are conditional theoretical references, not experimental observations or mandatory outcomes. Their isolated aluminum-cluster baseline uses Gaussian16/PBE0/def2-SVP, searches over binding positions and spin multiplicities, same-level frequencies without imaginary modes, and zero-point corrections (SI Computational details). The source uses Hirshfeld charge, ESP, MO and CDA analyses with Multiwfn/VMD to support its interpretation. Reproduce the relevant disclosed baseline or justify scientifically supported substitutions and delimit comparability. Merely reproducing an AEA number does not establish the electronic explanation. PBE-D3/PAW calculations and 300-K 10-ps AIMD are separate source stability studies; graphene confinement and other metallic cores are outside this bounded task. Complete shell assignment is needed only if that claim is made; structural retention or a LUMO shift alone does not prove it. V1 frozen-bare-core, matched vertical-density and prescribed state/control matrices were benchmark additions, not an obligatory author protocol. The actual SI has structural figures but no complete Cartesian table; construct starts and document uncertainty instead of claiming exact source-coordinate reproduction.
