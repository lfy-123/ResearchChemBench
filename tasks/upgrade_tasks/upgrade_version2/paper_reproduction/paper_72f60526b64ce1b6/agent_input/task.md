# Scientific objective

Determine whether and under what conditions the bridge changes rectification within the supplied Group A molecules in Au(111) junctions. Develop and test a molecular-level explanation for the response, and establish how far the evidence supports it within the stated junction and bias domain.

# Author-provided scientific guidance

The authors study Group A phenyl-donor/pyrimidinyl-acceptor molecules with no bridge, ethene bridge or ethane bridge; Group B is a separate larger family outside this task. Their source interpretation attributes bridge-dependent rectification to electronic coupling and asymmetry. The reported Group A A–D and A–pi–D currents are largely symmetric over the examined domain; the overall source trend favors sigma bridges for rectification and pi bridges for current. These are author claims to test, not required conclusions. Isolated structures use Gaussian16 Rev.B.01/B3LYP/6-31+G(d), terminal S constraints and field response, with B3LYP/6-311++G(d,p) property calculations. Actual junctions use QuantumATK W-2024.09 NEGF+DFT/PBE, Au(111) semi-infinite electrodes (3x3x2 source cells), four central-region Au layers on either side, fixed bulk electrode atoms and relaxed molecule/interfaces to 0.05 eV/angstrom, SZP(Au)/DZP(other atoms), 4x4x130 source k sampling and 75-Hartree mesh cutoff. The source examines -2 to +2 V. Reproduce the relevant disclosed protocol where available and account explicitly for substitutions or ambiguities. Its printed Landauer integrand omits the second Fermi-function symbol; use the physical difference f_L-f_R. The isolated-field statement in volts does not by itself specify an electric-field strength: a potential-drop length and units must be justified. Source optimized SI coordinates are withheld in this matched task; independently prepared starts and departures from the source geometry must be documented. SIESTA/TranSIESTA/TBtrans is an available alternative interface, not the author software or a certified numerical equivalent. Molecular reversal and a fixed unilateral displacement were V1 benchmark additions, not source-mandated or V2-required experiments.
