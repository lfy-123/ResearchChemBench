# Author route and expanded benchmark scope

The authors use B3LYP/6-31G(d,p) in Gaussian09 and NTO analysis in Multiwfn3.8, and attribute TADF differences to donor substitution and excited-state character. Main Figure1 and SI synthesis headings/HRMS define the structures. SI theoretical methods prints 6-31(d), and some synthesis reagent/product labels conflict with the correctly named headings and formulas; do not silently interpret these as new molecules. The common torsion, independent conformer and direct SOC comparisons are new benchmark tests. Device EQE is outside this molecular calculation.
