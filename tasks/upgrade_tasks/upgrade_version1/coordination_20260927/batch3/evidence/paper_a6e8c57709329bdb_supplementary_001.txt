Figure S3. FTIR Spectrum of Complex Mn-L
Characteristic bands for v(O–H/νN–H) at around 3400 cm⁻¹, ν(C=O) at about 1650–1700 cm⁻¹,

and ν(C=N) stretching of the azomethine group at about 1610 cm⁻¹ are visible in the HL ligand's

FTIR spectrum. These bands move to lower frequencies when complexed with Mn, indicating

that the azomethine nitrogen and carbonyl oxygen coordinate with the Mn core. Furthermore,

the Mn–L complex exhibits novel absorptions in the 600–500 cm⁻¹ range, which are ascribed

to stretching vibrations between Mn–N and Mn–O. These spectrum shifts verify that the Mn–

L combination was successfully coordinated and formed.
