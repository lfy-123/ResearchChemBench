 Analysis of norDTCO structure.

      There are also several other interesting features of the molecular structure of

norDTCO. The C5-C6 and C4-C5 bond distances (1.51 Å) are close to the C-C bond

distance in cyclopropane (1.51 Å) 2 and the cyclopropane ring of nortricyclene (1.515 Å)

3 but the C5-C7 bond length in norDTCO is significantly longer (1.545 Å). The C4-C3-

C9 angle decreased to 98.4° from the 101.5° corresponding angle in nortricyclene 3. The C4-

C3-C8 angle was found to be 106.6° in the DFT structure.

      The C3-C9-C6, C3-C4-C5, and C3-C8-C7 bond angles in norDTCO (97.0, 97.3° and

97.1, respectively) are generally smaller than the corresponding bond angles in nortricyclene

(97°, 104.6 and 107.0, respectively) 3. However, the S1-C8-C3 and S2-C4-C3 bond angles

in norDTCO are remarkably large (124°). These angles are larger than the corresponding

angles in nordithia-1 (2-endo-6-endo-bis(methylthio)3-exo-5-exo-dichloro[2.2.1]heptane)
