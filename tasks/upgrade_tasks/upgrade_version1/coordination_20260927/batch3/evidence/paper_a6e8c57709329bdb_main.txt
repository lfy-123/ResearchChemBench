                                          Conclusions

                                                                       In conclusion, we have synthesized and structurally described a
                                                     Mn–L complex whose supramolecular assembly involving C–
                                           H  p and p  p interactions stabilizes its crystal lattice. Due to
                                                                             ligand-to-ligand charge transfer (LLCT) and less orbital overlap,
                                                                       the Mn–L complex exhibits a hypsochromically shifted violet
                                                                    emission at 411 nm with a lower quantum yield of 0.61 than the
                                                                                 free ligand, which has an emission band at 433.6 nm with a
                                                          quantum yield of 0.66. Its exceptional selectivity toward Fe(III)
                                                                         over other metal ions is confirmed by the HL ligand’s ability to
                                                                        operate as a highly selective fluorescent probe for Fe(III), where
                                                                       coordination generates dramatic emission quenching with a
                                                                  low detection limit (0.76 mM) and high binding constant (2.74
                                                                       105 M  1). According to complementary theoretical evaluations,
