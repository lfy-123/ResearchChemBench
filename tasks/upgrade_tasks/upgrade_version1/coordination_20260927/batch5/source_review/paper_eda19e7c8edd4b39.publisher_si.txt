Supplementary Fig. 4. Secondary electron imaging and site-specific spectroscopy revealing
the formation and electronic signature of a γ'-Ni3Al island on the β-NiAl(100) surface during
UHV annealing of β-NiAl(100) at 800 °C. (a) Spatially resolved image showing the formation of
a γ'-Ni3Al island (cyan) during UHV annealing of β-NiAl(100) at 800 °C. The image was acquired
using secondary electrons generated by X-ray illumination (photon energy = 853.4 eV). (b)
Site-specific Ni L3-edge NEXAFS spectra collected from the γ'-Ni3Al island region (cyan) and
the island-free NiAl(100) surface (magenta) at 800 °C. The star highlights a new low-energy
peak corresponding to γ'-Ni3Al. Spectra are vertically offset for visual clarity.

=== PDF PAGE 7 ===
Supplementary Fig. 6. XPS of a clean β-NiAl(100) surface taken at 800 °C under UHV before
the formation of γ'-Ni3Al precipitates, showing the variations in the Al-to-Ni ratio in the bulk
and subsurface regions as probed at photon energies of 180 eV, 400 eV, and 800 eV. These
results indicate a thermodynamic preference for Al at the surface on the β-NiAl(100) matrix.
