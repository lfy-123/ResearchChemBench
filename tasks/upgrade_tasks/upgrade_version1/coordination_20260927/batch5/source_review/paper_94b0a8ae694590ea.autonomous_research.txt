# Private paper route

## 1. Scientific objective and author claim

The paper uses DFT frontier-orbital energies to explain why three TTA derivatives modulate p-type doping and thermoelectric behavior of SWCNT composites. The authors claim that the alkylphenyl derivative has relatively high frontier levels and favorable interfacial interactions.

## 2. System and model boundary

The isolated neutral singlet molecules are 2BT-TTA, 2BF-TTA, and 2(C8Ph)-TTA. The supplied SI optimized standard orientations define the molecular geometries. The reported calculation is molecular DFT; SWCNTs enter only as an external energy-alignment reference.

## 3. Authors' implemented computational route

| Step | Purpose | Input | Method/software | Key parameters | Output | Source evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Obtain relaxed isolated-molecule structures | 2BT-TTA, 2BF-TTA, 2(C8Ph)-TTA models | Gaussian09W DFT optimization | B3LYP/6-31G(d), neutral singlets | optimized geometry | ev_doc_2d4c711e9554_000134_79264b09e072; SI Tables S1–S3 |
| 2 | Compute frontier orbitals | optimized geometries | Gaussian09W DFT orbital calculation | B3LYP/6-31G(d) | HOMO/LUMO energies and orbital distributions | ev_doc_2d4c711e9554_000134_79264b09e072; ev_doc_2d4c711e9554_000162_0a3743430518 |
| 3 | Relate levels to doping | calculated levels and SWCNT HOMO reference | energy-level comparison | SWCNT HOMO reference stated in paper | qualitative p-type-doping interpretation | ev_doc_2d4c711e9554_000155_782f5937103b; ev_doc_2d4c711e9554_000164_a19fc3681a67; ev_doc_2d4c711e9554_000177_8f4b74c184a8 |

## 4. Validation and analysis protocol

The paper reports the three optimized geometries in SI Tables S1–S3 and compares the calculated frontier levels across substituents and with the SWCNT HOMO. The interpretation is qualitative: higher molecular HOMO levels favor electron transfer to SWCNTs and p-type doping; the higher LUMO is described as reducing undesired electron migration.

## 5. Private reference results

HOMO (eV), in the order 2BT-TTA, 2BF-TTA, 2(C8Ph)-TTA: -5.04, -5.00, -4.99. LUMO: -2.14, -2.08, -1.65. The 2(C8Ph)-TTA molecule is highest for both HOMO and LUMO. The paper states the SWCNT HOMO as -5.060 eV.

## 6. Limitations and interpretation boundaries

Frontier-orbital energies are method- and geometry-dependent one-electron quantities, not direct observables of a composite. The calculation does not model explicit SWCNT interfaces, solvent, temperature, aggregation, or charge transfer. The alignment is therefore a qualitative explanatory model.
