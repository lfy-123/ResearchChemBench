1. Experimental Section
1.1 Synthesis of organic small molecules.
     Thieno[2',3':4,5]thieno[3,2-b]thieno[2,3-d]thiophene (TTA)





    Using 3,6-dibromo-2,5-diiodothieno[3,2-b]thiophene (1.6 g, 2.9 mmol, 1 equivalent),

trimethylsilyl acetylene (626.5 mg, 6.38 mmol, 2.2 equivalents), CuI (44 mg, 0.232 mmol, 8%

mol), and Pd(PPh3)2Cl2 (81.4 mg, 0.116 mmol, 4% mol) were added to DIPEA/THF (30 mL/30

mL) and stirred at room temperature for 12 hours. The residual crude product was purified by

flash silica gel column chromatography (eluted with petroleum ether) to afford a yellow solid

((3,6-dibromothieno[3,2-b]thiophene-2,5-diyl)bis(ethyne-2,1-diyl))bis(trimethylsilane) (1.14 g,

2.34  mmol,  81%).  Then   ((3,6-dibromothieno[3,2-b]thiophene-2,5-diyl)bis(ethyne-2,1-

diyl))bis(trimethylsilane) (98 mg, 0.2 mmol, 1 equivalent), MeCOSK (69 mg, 0.6 mmol, 3

equivalents), CuTC (4 mg, 0.02 mmol, 10 mol%), and PivONa (62 mg, 0.5 mmol, 2.5

equivalents) were added to DMAc (3 mL) and reacted at 150°C for 48 hours. The residual crude

product was purified by flash silica gel column chromatography (eluted with petroleum ether)

to afford the white solid compound TTA (33 mg, 0.13 mmol, 65%).1H NMR (400 MHz, CDCl3):

δ 7.37 (d, J = 5.2 Hz, 2H), 7.32 (d, J = 5.2 Hz, 2H). 13C{1H} NMR (101 MHz, CDCl3): δ 140.3,

132.7, 132.0, 126.1, 120.9. HRMS (ESI): m/z calcd for C10H4S4 [M]+ 251.9190, found 251.9184.


     2,6-dibromothieno[2',3':4,5]thieno[3,2-b]thieno[2,3-d]thiophene (2Br-TTA)




    To a solution of TTA (334 mg, 1.32 mmol, 1 equiv.) in a mixture of chloroform and acetic

acid (v:v = 1:1, 100 mL), portions of NBS (470 mg, 2.64 mmol, 2 equiv.) were added at 0 ℃

over 30 min. The mixture was stirred for 2 hours. The residue was obtained after removal of

the solvent and washed with water and then with acetone three times to remove the starting

material as well as the monosubstituted byproduct. Recrystallization from toluene gave 2Br-

TTA (414 mg, 1.01 mmol, 78%) as grey solid. 1H NMR (400 MHz, C2D2Cl4): δ 7.33 (s, 2H).

HRMS (ESI): m/z calcd for C10H2Br2S4: [M]+ 407.7401, found 407.7402.

    2Br-TTA (205 mg, 0.5 mmol, 1 equiv.) and 2-tributylstannylbenzo[b]thiophene (529 mg,

1.25 mmol, 2.5 equiv.) were put into a 25 mL Schlenk tube, evacuated and refilled with nitrogen

three times, then anhydrous toluene (10 mL) was added and degassed with nitrogen for 20

minutes. Next, Pd(PPh3)4 (58 mg, 0.05 mmol, 10 mol%) was added under the protection of

nitrogen, and the reaction mixture was stirred at 100 ℃ for 24 hours. The residue was obtained

sublimation to afford 2BT-TTA (155 mg, 0.3 mmol, 60%) as orange solid. Anal. calcd for

2,6-bis(benzo[b]furan-2-yl)thieno[2',3':4,5]thieno[3,2-b]thieno[2,3-d]thiophene    (2BF-

TTA)





    2Br-TTA (205 mg, 0.5 mmol, 1 equiv.) and 2-tributylstannylbenzo[b]furan (509 mg, 1.25

mmol, 2.5 equiv.) were put into a 25 mL Schlenk tube, evacuated and refilled with nitrogen

three times, then anhydrous toluene (10 mL) was added and degassed with nitrogen for 20

minutes. Next, Pd(PPh3)4 (58 mg, 0.05 mmol, 10 mol%) was added under the protection of

nitrogen, and the reaction mixture was stirred at 100 ℃ for 24 hours. The residue was obtained

after removal of the solvent and washed with acetone and toluene sequentially. Vacuum

sublimation to afford 2BF-TTA (152 mg, 0.314 mmol, 63%) as orange solid. Anal. calcd for

C26H12O2S4 (%): C, 64.44; H, 2.50; S, 26.46. Found: C, 64.51; H, 2.47; S, 26.93. MS (MALDI):

calcd for C26H12O2S4 [M]+ 483.972, found 483.837.

flask, evacuated and refilled with nitrogen three times, then toluene (30 mL), ethanol (20 mL)

and water (10 mL) were added and degassed with nitrogen for 40 minutes. Next, Pd(PPh3)4 (58

1.2 Preparation of n-Type SWCNTs film[1].
5 mg SWCNTs and 100 mg PEI were dispersed in 25 mL of methanol. The resulting suspension
was sonicated using an ultrasonic homogenizer (JY96-11N, Xinzi Biotechnology Co., Ltd.,
China) in an ice-water bath at 65 W for 60 min to prevent overheating. After sonication, the
PEI/SWCNT composite films were obtained by vacuum filtration. The films were frozen with
liquid nitrogen and subsequently dried in a vacuum freeze dryer at −60 °C for 24 h to yield the
dried n-type SWCNT composite films. The resulting n-type PEI/SWCNT composite films
exhibit an electrical conductivity of 2477.39 ± 247.55 S cm−1 and a Seebeck coefficient of
−30.58 ± 1.68 μV K−1, corresponding to a PF of 233.21 ± 48.56 μW m−1 K−2 at room temperature.
