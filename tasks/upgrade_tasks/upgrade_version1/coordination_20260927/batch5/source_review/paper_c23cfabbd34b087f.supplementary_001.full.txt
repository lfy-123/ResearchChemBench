General


Chemical reagents were purchased from Sigma–Aldrich Co. Canada, Oakwood Products, Inc.,

Alfa Aesar Co. or TCI America Co. and were used as received. Vat Orange 1 was purchased from

Hangzhou Chungyo Chemical Co. Ltd. and vat Orange 3 was a courtesy from Heubach GmbH as

Monolite Red 316801 product.



Apparatus


NMR spectra were recorded using a Varian Inova AS400 spectrometer (Varian, Palo Alto, USA)

at 400 MHz or an Agilent DD2 500 MHz. Signals are reported as m (multiplet), s (singlet), d

(doublet), t (triplet), dd (doublet of doublet), and br s (broad singlet), and coupling constants are

reported in hertz (Hz). The chemical shifts are reported in ppm () relative to residual solvent peak

or TMS. High-resolution mass spectra (HRMS) were recorded using a Agilent 6210 time-of-flight

(TOF) LCMS apparatus equipped with an APPI ion source (Agilent Technologies, Toronto,

Canada).  UV-visible  spectra  of  monomers  were  recorded  on  a  Varian  diode-array

spectrophotometer  (model  Cary  7000)  using  10 mm  path  length  quartz  cells.  Cyclic

voltammograms (CVs) were recorded on a BioLogic SP-50e Single channel potentiostat using

platinum wires as working and counter electrodes at a scan rate of 50 mV s-1 and a Ag/Ag+ (0.01

M of AgNO3 in dichloromethane) reference electrode in an anhydrous and nitrogen-saturated

solution of 0.1 M of tetrabutylammonium hexafluorophosphate (Bu4NPF6) in dry dichloromethane

at room temperature. In these conditions, for a freshly prepared reference electrode, the half-wave

oxidation potential (E½) of ferrocene should be around 0.475 V versus Ag/Ag+.1, 2 The highest

occupied molecular orbital (HOMO) and the lowest unoccupied molecular orbital (LUMO) were

Electrochemistry

The ferrocene/ferrocenium couple was recorded by CV to establish the zero-potential reference

(half-wave potential: -0.35 V) at room temperature, and the initial potential was set to -1.0 V. The

analytical window was adjusted accordingly for DPV measurements, which served to determine

the vertex potentials for CV. Between DPV and CV runs, the cell was purged with nitrogen and

the  electrodes were flame-cleaned. Oxidation  processes were measured from  -1.0 V by

incrementally increasing the vertex potential in successive CV cycles, while reduction processes

were recorded after a second deoxygenation and electrode cleaning. During data analysis, all

experimental results were corrected by shifting the curves so that the ferrocene/ferrocenium redox

couple corresponded to 0.475 V. The electroactive surface area of the platinum wire electrodes

was not determined, as varying immersion depths were employed during the experiments.

Computational Details

All the calculations reported in this paper were obtained with the GAUSSIAN 09 suite of

            7                                                                                         8
programs. Electron correlation was partially taken into account using the (u)B3LYP  functional

                                                                                              9
in conjunction with the D3 dispersion correction suggested by Grimme et al. and the double-

quality plus  polarization functions def2-SVP10  basis  set  for  all atoms. All species were

characterized by frequency calculations,11 and have positive definite Hessian matrices.

The aromaticity of the different species was assessed by means of the Nuclear Independent

Chemical Shift (NICS)12 calculations using the gauge invariant atomic orbital (GIAO)13 method

at the B3LYP/def2-SVP level using the optimized B3LYP-D3/def2-SVP geometries. In addition,

the Clar sextets were visualized using the electron density of delocalized bonds (EDDB)14 method.

Calculations of the absorption spectrum were accomplished using time-dependent density

functional theory (TD-DFT)15 at the same DFT level using the optimized geometries. The

assignment of the excitation energies to the experimental bands was performed on the basis of the

energy values and oscillator strengths. The B3LYP Hamiltonian was chosen because it was proven

to provide reasonable UV-vis spectra for a variety of chromophores.16 Cartesian coordinates (in

Å) and total energies (in a.u., ZPVE included) of all the stationary points discussed in the text. All
