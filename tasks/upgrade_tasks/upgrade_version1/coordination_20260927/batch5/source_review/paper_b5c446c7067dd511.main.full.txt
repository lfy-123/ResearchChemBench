Study on optical properties of phenanthroimidazole-based HLCT emitters
with extended conjugation
Xiao Song , Shengbo Zhu *   , Yongliang Liu , Qianqian Zhao
Shaanxi Key Laboratory of Photoelectric Functional Materials and Devices, School of Materials and Chemical Engineering, Xi’an Technological University, Xi’an 710021,
China.


A R T I C L E  I N F O                  A B S T R A C T

Keywords:                                       Designing fluorescent molecules with efficient deep blue emission both in solid state and in solution (Dual-state
Phenanthrimidazole                                 emission: DSE) has always been a challenging research topic for researchers. Achieving molecules with spatial
Hybridized local and charge transfer (HLCT)           configurations and excellent photophysical properties remains an enduring challenge. Here, four D-A type deep-
Anthracene                                                blue fluorescent molecules with hybridized local and charge-transfer (HLCT) characteristics using methyl-pyrene                                               modified phenanthroimidazole as the donor core and various aromatic groups as acceptors are developed.Dual-state emission
                                               Naphthalene, anthracene, and pyrene groups with wide energy gap (Eg) and large ΔET1-T2 values ensured deep-
                                                blue emission while blocking T2→T1 channels. The mP-series study elucidated how different functional groups
                                                   influence optoelectronic properties. We have effectively confirmed through relevant experiments that triplet
                                                   excitons are utilized through the high reverse intersystem crossing process (High-RISC) process. Among them,
                                    An-mP and Py-mP display high fluorescence quantum yields (PLQY), with values of 80.2 % and 84.7 % in the
                                                      solid state and 87.9 % and 84.4 % in the THF solution, respectively, which make both promising candidates for
                                                         efficient DSE materials. Besides, except for Ph-mP which exhibits a narrow full width at half maxima (FWHM)
                                               but low PLQY, An-mP exhibits relatively narrow FWHM and relatively high PLQY in the majority of solutions in
                                                 the solid state, which has important guiding significance for constructing high color purity and efficient emission
                                     HLCT molecules.
