Optimization of metalation reagent

Table S1: The conditions and results of optimization in batch reactor.





In a dried-up test tube, benzyl chloromethyl sulfide (43.0 mg, 0.25 mmol) in THF (5 mL), MeOH

(24.0 mg, 0.75 mmol, 3.0 equiv) and metalation reagent (0.75 mmol, 3.0 equiv) were added and stirred

for 10 min. After stirring the solution was quenched by saturated NH4Cl aq (condition A). Otherwise,

metalation reagent (0.75 mmol, 3.0 equiv) was added to benzyl chloromethyl sulfide (43.0 mg, 0.25

mmol) in THF 5 mL. After stirring specified minutes, MeOH (24.0 mg, 0.75 mmol, 3.0 equiv) was

added to the solution to quench the reaction and stirred for 10 min. Saturated NH4Cl aq was added to

the solution to completely quench the reaction (condition B). Undecane (30 μL) was added to the

solution as an internal standard. The yields were determined by gas chromatography.
