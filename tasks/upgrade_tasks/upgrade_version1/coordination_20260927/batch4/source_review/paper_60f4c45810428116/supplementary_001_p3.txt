2.   Experimental Section


    Preparation of lithium naphthalenide (LiNp)

      Lithium wire (1.0 equiv) was cut off about 1 to 2 mm pieces into a dried-up flask. Then naphthalene

     (1.3 equiv) and anhydrous THF were put into the flask. After stirring for 2 hours and becoming dark

     green, they were used to experiments.
