DFT-Optimized Structure of triaminephosphines





Figure S1. DFT computation optimized structures of triaminephosphines with different structures.
Ligand properties were quantified using Tolman electronic parameters (TEP) and cone angles (θ). Lower
TEP values indicate stronger nucleophilicity, while larger θ values denote greater steric bulk. TEP
measurements revealed: (Me2N)3P (2063.0 cm-1) > (Et2N)3P (2056.1 cm-1) > Py3P (2050.2 cm-1),
indicating nucleophilicity order: Py3P  (Et2N)3P  (Me2N)3P. Cone angles (steric bulk) decreased as:
(Et2N)3P (134°) > Py3P (130°) > (Me2N)3P (124°).
