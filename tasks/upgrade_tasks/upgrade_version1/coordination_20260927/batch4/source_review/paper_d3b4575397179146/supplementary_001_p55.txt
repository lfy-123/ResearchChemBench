4.3  Products of oxidation

General procedure B: N-methyl oxidation





                          Scheme S2. General procedure for N-methyl oxidation.

The N-methyl compound (1 equiv.) and dFHeptZ (2.5–10 mol%) were dissolved in MeCN (0.1 M). The

atmosphere was replaced with oxygen (balloon) and the reaction mixture was irradiated at 456 nm

using set-up A (0 °C) or set-up B (rt). After reaction completion, the solvent was evaporated and the

crude product was purified (often by flash column chromatography on silica gel, purification method

is indicated for each compound) to afford the N-formyl product.
