N-isoPropyl-N-methylpivalamide (1i)





To a rt solution of i-PrNH2 (4.0 mmol, 1 equiv.) in CH2Cl2 (20 mL, 0.2 M) was added PivCl (4.4 mmol, 1.1

equiv.) followed by Et3N (2 mL) and the resulting mixture was stirred at rt for 2 h. Brine was added,

followed by CH2Cl2 and the phases were separated. The aqueous phase was extracted with CH2Cl2. The

combined organic phases were dried over Na2SO4, filtered, and carefully concentrated in vacuo

(product is volatile) to afford the amide which was used in the next step without further purification.


The crude residue from previous step was dissolved in THF (40 mL, 0.1 M) and cooled to 0 °C. NaH

(60 w%, 8.0 mmol, 2 equiv.) was carefully added and the resulting solution was stirred at 0°C for 15

min. Methyl iodide (8.0 mmol, 2 equiv.) was added dropwise at 0°C, and the reaction mixture was

stirred at rt for 16 h. Brine was added, followed by CH2Cl2 and the phases were separated. The aqueous

phase was extracted with CH2Cl2. The combined organic phases were dried over Na2SO4, filtered, and

concentrated in vacuo. The crude residue was purified by flash column chromatography on silica gel

(EtOAc/Petroleum ether) to afford 1i (301 mg, 1.92 mmol, 48%) as a colorless oil.
