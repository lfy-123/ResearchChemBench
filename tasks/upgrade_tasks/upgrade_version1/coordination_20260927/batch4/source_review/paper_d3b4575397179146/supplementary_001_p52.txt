1-(tert-butyl)-3-cyclopentyl-1-methylurea (5c)





To a 0 °C solution of N-tert-Butylmethylamine (241 µL, 2.0 mmol, 1.0 equiv.) in CH2Cl2 (20 mL, 0.1 M)

was added tert-butyle isocyanate (0.23 mL, 2.0 mmol, 1.0 equiv.), followed by Et3N (0.42 mL, 3.0 mmol,

1.5 equiv.) and the resulting mixture was stirred at rt for 4 h. Brine was added, followed by CH2Cl2 and

the phases were separated. The aqueous phase was extracted with CH2Cl2. The combined organic

phases were dried over Na2SO4, filtered, and concentrated in vacuo. The residue was purified by flash

column chromatography on silica gel (EtOAc/Petroleum ether) to afford 5c (390 mg, 1.99 mmol, 99%)

as a white solid.

1H NMR (500 MHz, CDCl3): δ 4.18 (s, 1H), 4.05 (sext., J = 6.8 Hz, 1H), 2.78 (s, 3H), 2.00 – 1.93 (m, 2H),

1.68 – 1.60 (m, 2H), 1.60 – 1.53 (m, 2H), 1.37 (s, 9H), 1.37 – 1.29 (m, 2H);

13C NMR (126 MHz, CDCl3): δ 159.3, 55.5, 52.4, 33.8 (2C), 32.0, 29.1 (3C), 23.8 (2C);

HRMS (ESI+, m/z): Calculated for C11H23N2O+ [M+H]+: 199.1805, found 199.1800.



Methyl N-(cyclopentylcarbamoyl)-N-methylalaninate (5e)

3.0 mmol, 1.5 equiv.) and the resulting mixture was stirred at rt for 4 h. Brine was added, followed by

CH2Cl2 and the phases were separated. The aqueous phase was extracted with CH2Cl2. The combined

organic phases were dried over Na2SO4, filtered, and concentrated in vacuo. The residue was purified

by flash column chromatography on  silica gel (EtOAc/Petroleum ether) to afford 5e (373 mg,

1.64 mmol, 82%) as a colorless oil.
