4.1  Synthesis of starting materials

Compounds 2g, 2h, and 3a are commercially available. Compounds 1a,3 1b,4 1e,5 3c,6 3d,7 5a,8 5d,8 7a,9

7b,9 and 7c9 were prepared according to the literature.


N-(4-Cyanophenyl)-N-methylpivalamide (1c)





To a rt solution of 4-cyano-N-methylaniline (0.40 g, 2.0 mmol, 1 equiv.) in CH2Cl2 (10 mL, 0.2 M) was

added PivCl (2.2 mmol, 1.1 equiv.), followed by Et3N (1 mL) dropwise. The reaction mixture was stirred

at rt for 4 h. Brine was added, the phases were separated and the aqueous phase was extracted with

CH2Cl2. The combined organic layers were dried with Na2SO4, filtered, and concentrated under reduced

pressure. The residue was purified by flash column chromatography on silica gel (EtOAc/Petroleum

ether gradient) to afford 1c (401 mg, 1.86 mmol, 89%).

1H NMR (400 MHz, CDCl3): δ 7.69 (d, J = 8.5 Hz, 2H), 7.33 (d, J = 8.5 Hz, 2H), 3.23 (s, 3H), 1.07 (s, 9H);

13C NMR (126 MHz, CDCl3): δ 178.1, 149.7, 133.4 (2C), 129.4 (2C), 118.2, 111.5, 41.1, 41.0, 29.4 (3C);

HRMS (ESI+, m/z): calculated for C13H17N2O+ [M+H]+: 217.1335, found 217.1328



Ethyl 4-(N-methylpivalamido)benzoate (1d)





To a rt solution of ethyl 4-aminobenzoate (0.33 g, 2.0 mmol, 1 equiv.) in CH2Cl2 (10 mL, 0.2 M) was

added PivCl (2.2 mmol, 1.1 equiv.), followed by Et3N (1 mL) dropwise. The reaction mixture was stirred

at rt for 4 h. Brine was added, the phases were separated and the aqueous phase was extracted with

CH2Cl2. The combined organic layers were dried with Na2SO4, filtered, and concentrated under reduced

pressure. The residue was purified by flash column chromatography on silica gel (EtOAc/Petroleum

ether gradient) to afford ethyl 4-pivalamidobenzoate (453 mg, 1.82 mmol, 91%) which was

immediately engaged in the next step.
