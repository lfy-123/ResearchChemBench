# Author route and expanded benchmark scope

The source uses B3LYP-D3/def2-SVP structures/frequencies and TD calculations with chloroform PCM. SI coordinates define 1M/2M methoxy and silyl-ethynyl computational models and include closed/open-shell alternatives. The authors relate extension to open-shell/optical changes. The benchmark adds consistent state competition and frozen-scaffold tests; it does not equate the different formulas of 1M and 2M.

## Expanded development scope

Determine how backbone topology and substituent changes affect open-shell character and low-energy optical states, separating relaxation and electronic effects with matched scaffolds and spin diagnostics.
