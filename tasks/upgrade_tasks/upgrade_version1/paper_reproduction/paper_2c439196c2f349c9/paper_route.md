# Author route and expanded benchmark scope

The authors discuss strong CW nonlinear response primarily as thermal lensing. Main p3 defines Z-scan/SSPM phase and absorption quantities; main pp9–10 describes time-dependent rings and compares the two optical techniques. The isolated INP B3LYP/6-311G(d,p) frontier-orbital argument in the source does not resolve thermal/electronic contributions. The benchmark joint model comparison, shared-parameter constraints and withheld-condition test are new and must use measured points, not the authors fitted constants.

## Expanded development scope

Test instantaneous electronic, thermal-memory and mixed models against the same time/power observations for the INP solutions under the reported experimental conditions, and determine whether their contributions are identifiable.
