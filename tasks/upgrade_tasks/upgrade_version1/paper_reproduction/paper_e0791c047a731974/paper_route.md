# Author route and expanded benchmark scope

Main pp4–6 and SI pp7–8 use Gaussian16 B3LYP/6-31G(d,p), SMD chloroform; Se uses def2-TZVP and I uses SDD, with iodide removed. The authors propose heavy-atom-assisted sensitization and discuss Cy2 and rubrene upconversion. The benchmark requires actual SOC and an independent acceptor energy cycle, which are not replaced by qualitative atomic-number arguments or the old isolated Cy2 energies.

## Expanded development scope

Compare actual state character and SOC in the three sensitizers and determine whether a consistently defined rubrene energy-transfer cycle supports their proposed sensitization role.
