# Submission guide

Submit `report/results.json` and `report/report.md`. The JSON records your research question, chosen models, actual operations, quantitative results, evidence, decisions, claims, validation and limitations. IDs and branch names are yours. Hypotheses may be added, revised or withdrawn; there is no required hypothesis count, predetermined explanation, control count or calculation matrix. A sound empirical investigation need not invent named hypotheses.

`models` must link atom-resolved identity files and explain mappings, composition, charge, spin, conditions and assumptions. New conformers, states, intermediates or finite models are allowed when physically admissible within the task's chemical scope. An input graph defines starting chemical identity; it does not prescribe geometry, a preferred endpoint or the only valid model. Justify changes of composition or state.

`operations` records the method and actual input/parameter and execution-log files, linked models, artifacts and failures. `results` contains numerical quantities with units, reference/state/condition definitions, uncertainty and extraction locators into raw products. Do not place unrelated quantities into a convenient schema field. The `other` quantity type needs a precise quantity/unit definition and receives the same physical and unit checks as named types. Every result, model, operation, claim and artifact reference must resolve uniquely.

Keep inputs, native outputs, structures, analyses and execution records under `outputs/`, `structures/`, `analysis/` or `logs/`; list their actual SHA256 in `artifacts`. Analysis of public observations is permitted, but paper numbers must remain labeled public evidence rather than new calculations. Generated analyses still need reproducible code, inputs and execution records. A formatted table or self-authored log alone cannot establish that a scientific calculation happened.

`decisions` stores concise purpose/prediction, action, observed evidence and resulting continuation/revision linked to execution records. Do not submit hidden chain of thought. Self-authored timestamps do not establish preregistration; authentic tool traces and artifact chronology support evidence provenance and earn no separate points. Report actual resources and unknown measurements honestly, and retain failed branches.

`claims` link numerical evidence to an inference and scope; `validations` state what was checked and whether it supports, limits or invalidates a claim. Evidence requirements follow the claims: a stationary-point claim requires defensible stationary-point evidence, an energetic comparison compatible state/composition/reference definitions, a spectral assignment physical state correspondence, and a causal conclusion a design capable of testing it. Reproduce the disclosed author baseline, or justify a scientifically comparable substitute and explain its consequences. Scientific findings require physically valid evidence. Private reference workflow steps earn no independent points.

`complete` is your claim of an adequately addressed question, subject to scientific review. `partial`, `bounded_failure` and `input_blocked` preserve useful work without asserting completion. Only established final propositions earn their assigned points; unresolved scientific conflicts fail the affected item; missing work or convergence failure alone cannot establish non-identifiability. Failure/blocked submissions require evidence and concrete recovery/release conditions. Format acceptance never awards scientific points. Report uncertainty and the limits of identification; no universal numerical tolerance is supplied for uncalibrated extensions.

## Scientific scoring

The 100-point score covers only independent final scientific answers listed in the task. Each outcome is judged independently as pass or fail; unresolved scientific conflicts fail only the affected outcome. Valid equivalent explanations pass directly. Missing evidence or invalid comparisons fail the affected outcome. Numerical values are evidence, without author-value matching windows. Given observations and disclosed source targets cannot earn credit by being copied. Native outputs, structures and validation support the conclusions without separate process points. The schema records provenance; record count, research chronology and report length are not scientific scoring gates.

The `decisions` array may be empty. A separate research chronology is optional; actual method, execution, result, native-artifact and claim-validity evidence remain required for the scientific results.
