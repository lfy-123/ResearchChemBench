# Submission and evidence

Write `report/results.json` under the supplied JSON Schema and `report/report.md` as a readable scientific report. For this paper-reproduction mode, reproduce the disclosed author baseline or document a scientifically justified substitution and its consequences. Source the disclosed protocol defines the scientific reproduction comparison, with physically justified substitutions assessed on their results rather than process points. Use your own model names and design any additional investigations; no hypothesis count, fixed result panels or calculation count is prescribed. The flexible format does not waive the disclosed protocol. A research log is a concise record of useful decisions, observations and responses, not private internal reasoning. An empty model array is permitted where no explicit model was formulated; this does not establish a successful investigation.

Identify the actual molecular systems, electronic states and environments used. Give unique IDs for systems, methods, records, results and claims. All references must resolve to those IDs. Each record identifies a real calculation or analysis and links workspace-relative `outputs/`, `data/` or `code/` artifacts with SHA256, their role and a precise section/line/table locator. Preserve native outputs, inputs and any analysis needed to reproduce derived numbers. A path or hash is not itself scientific evidence. Use tool job identifiers when available. Separate generated evidence from supplied facts or citations; never present a source number as a new calculation.

Every reported numerical quantity requires a unit, definition, object mapping and uncertainty statement; dimensionless quantities explicitly say so. Method-specific evidence belongs in additional named fields or linked artifacts. Report identities and stoichiometry for transformations, conventions for differences, and evidence appropriate to any claimed geometry, state or response. Failed, constrained and unvalidated structures must not be relabeled validated minima. A claim of a minimum requires stationarity and curvature evidence or a justified equivalent; a transition-state claim additionally needs relevant saddle/connectivity evidence. These are conditional validity requirements, not prescribed investigations.

Support/refutation claims link results; results link records; records link actual artifacts. State why the evidence discriminates the claimed explanation and what uncertainty or unexamined scope remains. A conclusion different from the author, or a bounded negative or undecidable result, should be reported when supported. It receives credit only for the independent scientific proposition it actually establishes, not as a generic substitute for a missing target. Missing work cannot be converted into evidence of absence or non-identifiability. Source agreement, fluent prose, raw job counts and filling every field do not establish success.

Use `complete` only for a substantively answered bounded question, including a demonstrated negative or unresolved answer. Its minimum format requires at least one completed calculation or analysis record and at least one numerical quantity in a record or result. This minimum does not establish adequate coverage or scientific success. `partial` and `bounded_failure` allow honest incomplete work and diagnostic evidence; list missing work. Schema acceptance validates format, not scientific completion. No calculation is required just to fill a failure record if the environment prevented it: report that limitation honestly. Untested assertions earn no result credit.

State whether each prediction or decision is prospective, retrospective or unsupported by an independent trace. Self-written dates do not establish ordering. All supplied data are visible; there is no blind held-out interface. Use only the tools and resource limits actually exposed by the runner; do not assume a software license, fixed CPU allowance or extra experimental access.

Scientific scoring uses paper_science_v3: 100 points across independent final conclusions, with each leaf receiving its assigned points or zero. There is no process score or multiplier. Investigative freedom and honest partial reporting remain permitted; credit requires actual evidence establishing the scientific proposition, and unsupported or differently scoped claims do not satisfy it.

Final answers are scored under paper_science_v3 / final_results_100. Each required scientific result must be supported by its relevant calculations or analysis; structures, executions and research records are evidence, not independent score items. Each result is judged independently as pass or fail; established equivalent explanations pass and unresolved scientific conflicts fail only the affected result. Numerical values are evidence, without author-value matching windows.

Research decision records may be an empty array, including for `complete` submissions. Their presence or number is not a scoring or completion requirement; conclusions still require the relevant scientific evidence.
