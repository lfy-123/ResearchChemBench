## Scientific scoring

The 100-point score covers only independent final scientific answers listed in the task. Each outcome is pass or fail; insufficient or scientifically invalid support fails the affected outcome. Valid equivalent explanations pass directly. Missing or scientifically invalid evidence fails the affected proposition; author-value matching windows do not apply. Given observations and disclosed source targets cannot earn credit by being copied. Native outputs, structures and validation support the conclusions without separate process points. The schema records provenance; record count, research chronology and report length are not scientific scoring gates.
