Submit report/results.json and report/report.md. Paths in the artifact index and scientific records must name real files below outputs/ or report/. Preserve native inputs/outputs, mappings and any analysis used to derive a result; list SHA256 hashes. JSON is an audit index, not a replacement for evidence.

Use your own names and number of models, investigations and comparisons. The models array may be empty when no explicit model is asserted. AR investigators choose their scientific route. PR investigators must reproduce the disclosed author baseline, or justify scientifically supported substitutions and explain which aspects remain comparable; additional investigation is independently designed. The common scientific-result standard does not require a private reference matrix, a prescribed number of hypotheses or the author's answer. A single well-founded explanation can be sufficient; a long list without discriminating evidence is not.

quantitative_observations records values with units, dimensions, method, object identifiers, reference state, uncertainty and evidence links. Report unquantified uncertainty honestly and explain its consequences. claims separates supported, refuted, unresolved and withdrawn statements. The conclusion must remain inside addressed_scope and the evidence actually generated. A claim of scientific non-identifiability requires affirmative evidence about the information limits; unfinished calculations do not establish it.

research_record contains short auditable decisions and links to tool events and resulting artifacts, including meaningful revisions or retention of the investigation. It is not a request for private internal reasoning. Self-written timestamps or narratives do not establish that a prediction preceded its evidence. A successful investigation need not invent a failure or change of mind.

complete means the scientific question has been adequately answered within the declared domain, including a sufficiently demonstrated negative or unresolved answer. partial and bounded_failure require failure_report with real diagnostics, missing evidence and concrete conditions for continuation. An honest infrastructure/input failure may receive an objective-not-scorable assessment; it is not successful science. Never label fabricated or unavailable quantities as measured results.

Only the final scientific answers receive points. Actual computational evidence supports those answers; method execution, research process, job count and report completeness do not receive separate points. Disclosed author values must be reproduced by calculations, not copied.

Paper-specific records: state_records identifies each investigated composition, charge, multiplicity, total electron count, geometry, atom map, framework assessment and validity evidence. attachment_assessments is populated for electron-attachment energies you report and links their contributing states and energy components to quantitative_observations. It may be empty for a supported structural exclusion or other explanation that does not claim an attachment energy; that does not automatically answer the family-level question. electronic_structure_assessment explains the evidence for your interpretation and its limits. There is no fixed list of states, ligand sites, analysis methods or computation order. A schema-valid endpoint record alone cannot establish an equilibrium structure, ground state or family trend. Preserve native inputs/outputs and any wavefunction, density, geometry or analysis actually used to support a claim.

Completion and coverage: the full question concerns the effect of ligation across n = 0, 1 and 2, with structure/state and electron-acceptance evidence appropriate to the inference. A single n, one positive-frequency endpoint or one attachment scalar supplies only its supported partial evidence. Reducing addressed_scope cannot convert that subset into whole-task completion or full credit for unexamined family claims. Credit independently valid parts. A sufficiently investigated structural exclusion, reconstruction or scientific non-distinguishability may contribute to a complete answer; missing states, an unattempted composition or a failed run alone cannot. This does not prescribe a spin list, binding-site grid, method/control matrix or a particular winning structure.

Final results use a single 100-point score. Supporting calculations and validity evidence qualify each conclusion without independent points. Each conclusion is judged independently as pass or fail; supported equivalent explanations pass and unresolved material scientific conflicts fail only the affected conclusion. Numerical values are evidence, without author-value matching windows. Missing calculation or unsupported uncertainty is not a successful result.
