# Scientific objective

Using neutral singlet substrate 1a and one equivalent of the supplied phosphoric-acid catalyst, investigate the first intramolecular Friedel–Crafts cyclization leading to the two benzofuran regiochannels. Both ring-closing aromatic carbons are adjacent to the carbon bearing the ether tether. Here “ortho/4-hydroxybenzofuran” and “para/6-hydroxybenzofuran” refer instead to positions relative to the free phenolic OH: the attacked carbon is respectively one or three aromatic bonds from the OH-bearing carbon. The resulting products retain OH at benzofuran C4 or C6. Determine the validated relative Gibbs barriers, their signed difference (para minus ortho) in kcal/mol, and whether the calculated ordering explains the supplied experimental observation.

# Author-provided scientific guidance

The authors qualitatively propose that catalyst organization can favor the ortho channel by activating both the ketone and phenolic OH; independently test this proposal.

Use the authors' primary composite protocol for the quantitative comparison: optimize geometries and compute harmonic frequencies at B3LYP-D3/6-31+G(d), then evaluate B3LYP-D3/6-311+G(d,p) single-point electronic energies on the same geometries. Use an ultrafine integration grid (or a demonstrably equivalent grid) and SMD(toluene) throughout. To make the main comparison unambiguous, this task uses zero-damped D3 (`EmpiricalDispersion=GD3` in Gaussian), not D3(BJ), and unscaled harmonic thermochemistry at 298.15 K and 1 atm. For each state compute `G_composite = E_high + (G_low - E_low)`; the low-level correction includes the zero-point contribution and must not be added a second time. This protocol provides the comparison conditions, not the structures, energies or winning channel.
