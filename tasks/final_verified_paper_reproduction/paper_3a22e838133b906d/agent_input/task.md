# Scientific objective

Test the authors' qualitative hypothesis that a neutral and a monoanionic ortho-phenyl-phosphonate-borane model retain an intramolecular O(P)···B interaction and that dealkylation/anion formation changes the P–O and O(P)···B distances in the same qualitative direction as the corresponding crystallographic structures. Independently choose and document calculations; do not assume the authors' numerical results.

# Author-provided scientific guidance

**Author hypothesis or claim.**
The authors propose that the neutral and monoanionic models retain an intramolecular O(P)···B interaction, and that anion formation produces P–O and O(P)···B distance changes that largely follow the corresponding crystallographic trend. They further associate the largest P–O change with the oxygen at the dealkylation site.

**Candidate route or mechanism.**
Examine retention of the intramolecular O(P)···B contact while comparing the neutral model with the monoanionic model generated by dealkylation at one ester oxygen. Use the oxygen identities and the state-to-state distance changes to test whether charge formation and the altered substitution pattern provide a plausible structural explanation.

**Discriminating evidence.**
Use independently optimized geometries, stationary-point validation by vibrational analysis or an explicitly justified equivalent, and extraction of the three P–O distances plus the O(P)···B contact for both states. Distinguish the proposed trend by comparing signed state-to-state changes with the reported neutral-to-salt crystallographic boundary, while treating the comparison qualitatively.

A result is validated only when the optimization is converged and a frequency (or an explicitly justified equivalent stationary-point test) shows no imaginary frequencies; report failures rather than silently substituting unvalidated structures. Scientific completion requires both validated states, all four distances for each state, the state-to-state differences and their structural interpretation.
