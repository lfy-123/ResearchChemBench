# Scientific objective

Computationally validate the specified neutral singlet IrCl2(eta2-O2NO)(PPh3)2 bidentate-nitrato model and its nitrate-associated harmonic IR signature against the source experiment. Generate and test your own structural and vibrational explanations, independently choose the computational interpretation and search coverage, perform the calculations, validate the selected stationary point and vibrational assignments, and report a bounded conclusion. The public specification fixes the chemical connectivity; do not treat discovery of a different connectivity as a required outcome.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use `data/inputs/complex_specification.json`. It uniquely defines formula C36H30Cl2IrNO3P2, neutral charge, singlet multiplicity, Ir1/Cl1/Cl2/P1/P2/N1/O1/O2/O3 labels, two PPh3 ligands, and O1/O2 bidentate nitrate connectivity. The model is one isolated molecule; omit crystal packing, solvent, counterions and periodicity. You may construct 3D coordinates and choose software, relativistic treatment, basis, functional, convergence settings and frequency scaling. The measured comparison quantities are Ir1-O1/O2, N1-O1/O2/O3, O1-Ir1-O2, O1-N1-O2, O1-N1-O3, O2-N1-O3, and nitrate-associated IR frequencies. The experimental comparison is to molecular X-ray and KBr IR observations.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Generate and document at least one chemically sensible starting geometry; if alternative orientations or conformers are explored, retain their identities and validation context. Optimize the specified connectivity without silently changing protonation, charge, multiplicity or nitrate hapticity. Establish a genuine stationary point using a vibrational calculation or an explicitly justified equivalent; report imaginary frequencies and explain any unresolved saddle-point issue. Identify nitrate modes by displacement vectors or an equivalent mode-assignment procedure. Validate object identity by reporting the Ir-O and N-O bond identities and O1-Ir1-O2 bite angle. Compare observables with the source experimental observations and discriminate whether the computed evidence supports the bidentate assignment; distinguish calculation evidence from interpretation. Completion requires a reproducible calculation record, validated optimized structure or clearly documented bounded failure, and a final conclusion with limitations. Stop when the chosen search has exhausted the explicitly reported starting-geometry/conformer set and each advanced candidate has geometry and frequency validation, or when a reproducible resource/convergence barrier prevents completion; report coverage, stopping reason and best defensible partial result.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` conforming to `submission_schema.json`. Include independent rationale, methods, candidate/conformer records, stationary-point validation, named observables, nitrate mode assignments, comparison, conclusion and limitations. Include enough data for another researcher to identify every reported atom pair and mode. A bounded-failure branch is allowed only when it contains attempted calculations, diagnostics, coverage and an honest limitation.
