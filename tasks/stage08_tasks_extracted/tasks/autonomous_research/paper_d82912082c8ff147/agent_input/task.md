# Scientific objective

Independently discover and computationally characterize the relevant low-energy conformers of neutral gas-phase L-Ala3. Determine their relative free energies and normalized Boltzmann populations at 450 K, identify which conformer interconversions are plausibly enabled by low barriers in a seeded Ar supersonic-jet environment, and state what the calculations support about the experimentally relevant conformer distribution. The result must distinguish electronic-energy ordering, thermochemical population, and barrier-limited relaxation.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

The only molecular input is `data/inputs/ala3_system.json`, which gives the isomeric SMILES, formula, neutral charge, singlet multiplicity, and free-terminus connectivity for one isolated L-Ala3 molecule. The system boundary excludes solvent, counterions, complexes, covalent reactions, and protonation changes. The 450 K population is a gas-phase conformer thermochemistry calculation; the jet interpretation is qualitative and must report its barrier criterion and limitations. You may choose computational methods and software available in the environment, but must state them and units.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Generate a finite, explicitly described set of distinct conformer candidates from the public molecule. Retain candidate identity, generation source, stereochemical/connectivity checks, and a reproducible deduplication criterion. Advance candidates only when geometry optimization converges to the same molecular connectivity and a harmonic analysis supports a minimum (zero imaginary frequencies, or a clearly reported alternative validation with its limitation). Report search coverage, discarded duplicates, failed optimizations, and a stopping condition: stop when additional independent generation attempts produce no new validated minimum under the stated criterion, or report bounded failure and the coverage achieved.

For every retained conformer, report a unique identifier, coordinates or a submitted structure file, relative energy, thermochemical/free-energy definition at 450 K, and structural descriptors sufficient to interpret intramolecular hydrogen bonding. Normalize populations over the explicitly stated retained ensemble and disclose any omitted-state limitation. Generate and validate a finite set of plausible pairwise interconversion pathways among retained conformers; for each pathway identify endpoints by conformer ID, report the reaction coordinate or method, transition-state validation (one imaginary frequency along the connecting mode and endpoint connectivity), barrier definition and units, and apply an explicit jet-relaxation barrier criterion (an approximately 800 cm^-1 barrier scale may be used as a qualitative reference). If a pathway fails or is not attempted, preserve its endpoint IDs, method or reason, and limitation without fabricated numerical values. Stop pair searching when all retained conformer pairs have been attempted or a justified coverage boundary is reached. Compare your model to the qualitative experimental boundary that several Ala3 species can be relevant and that IR-compatible structures may not be uniquely distinguishable; do not claim an experimental population measurement from computation alone.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` conforming to `submission_schema.json`. It must include the validated conformer array, population analysis, pathway array, search/validation accounting, limitations, and a final conclusion. Include enough provenance for another researcher to reproduce the candidate generation, deduplication, calculations, and stopping decision. Numeric values must include units and method context; bounded failures must preserve the candidates and attempted validations rather than silently omitting them.
