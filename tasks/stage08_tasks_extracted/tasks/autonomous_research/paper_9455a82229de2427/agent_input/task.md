# Scientific objective

Investigate the isolated, single-collision reaction of ground-state SiN radical with isoprene and determine which neutral singlet SiNC5H7 product structures are computationally plausible after atomic-hydrogen loss. Generate and discriminate plausible product connectivities and mechanistic explanations independently. Report optimized product structures, relative 0 K reaction energies, and any intermediates or transition states used to support the conclusion. The measured endpoint is each candidate's reaction energy relative to separated SiN + isoprene, compared with the experimentally reported exoergicity window.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use `data/inputs/reactants.json`, which specifies neutral SiN radical `[Si]#N` (doublet), neutral isoprene `CC(=C)C=C` (singlet), the neutral singlet product formula SiNC5H7, atomic-H loss, and the single-collision collision-energy boundary. The scored system is the isolated molecule on its electronic potential-energy surface; do not add a host or solvent. You may generate 3D structures and choose computational methods, but state software, model chemistry, conformer treatment, and energy convention. The scored object is independently discovered product connectivity and its reaction energetics; do not assume a product structure from an internal label.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Define a finite candidate-generation procedure covering distinct SiNC5H7 connectivities and relevant conformers, and state how duplicate graphs are removed. Propose multiple plausible mechanistic hypotheses when more than one exists, then discriminate them with calculations. For every advanced candidate retain an unambiguous ID, structure file/SMILES, charge and multiplicity, and provenance. Optimize candidates and verify minima with a frequency calculation or a justified equivalent; report imaginary-frequency evidence. Compute the reaction energy against separately computed reactant energies using a stated consistent convention and units. If a transition state or intermediate is claimed, report its structure, frequency signature, and connectivity evidence (IRC or a scientifically justified alternative). Compare every validated candidate's energy with the experimentally reported exoergicity window, and distinguish energetic compatibility from proof of experimental branching. Do not infer or copy a hidden reference value; report calculated values and a qualitative compatibility assessment. Completion requires either (a) at least two independently generated, validated product candidates plus a reproducible comparison, or (b) a bounded-failure report documenting the explored generation space, validation failures, and why no candidate could be completed. Stop when the declared generation families and conformer/stationary-point searches are exhausted or when additional searches no longer produce a new graph or materially change the lowest validated energy; report coverage and limitations.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` conforming to the local `submission_schema.json`. Include candidate identities and structures, per-candidate validation and energies, reactant reference energies, reaction-energy comparison, mechanism evidence and discrimination where investigated, coverage/stopping statement, and a final conclusion. A bounded-failure branch is valid only with the required failure explanation and investigation log.
