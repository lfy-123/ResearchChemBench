# Submission guide

The submission contract records the investigation for this task. It records what you actually did; it does not prescribe a research matrix.

Report the static HRS quantities that support your comparison, their units and orientation convention, the actual molecular models and raw response/analysis artifacts. Retain enough numerical response data and executable transformations to reconstruct the reported observable. Link the explanation to evidence beyond merely ordering four scalar values. Any claimed geometric cause, numerical convergence or state assignment needs evidence specific to that claim; no particular diagnostic or intervention is compulsory.

Use free identifiers for your ideas, methods, models, executions, measurements and findings. Model source_system_id refers to a supplied identity; your model description may introduce physically justified states, conformers or representations within its boundary. Report composition, charge/spin, medium and every approximation. A finding may be supported, refuted, mixed, unresolved or withdrawn. There is no minimum number of ideas or required change of direction.

Each numerical measurement has observable, definition/reference, value (scalar or numeric array), unit, conditions, method/model references, uncertainty and evidence links. Use a numeric table artifact and an extraction/analysis script for spectra, tensors, ensembles or other large data. Do not hide the audit trail inside an unconstrained prose object. A missing number is not zero; omit an uncomputed measurement and explain it in incomplete_work. Uncalibrated uncertainty has kind=not_calibrated, magnitude=null and an explicit explanation; it does not excuse unwarranted precision or a unsupported complete claim.

Each evidence record identifies a workspace-relative file under outputs/, analysis/, structures/ or data/, SHA256, its kind and actual origin. Copy any public input used into the submission data/ with its original hash. Use unique IDs, resolve every link, and include raw native inputs/outputs for calculations or original data plus executable scripts for analyses. A plotted image or results.json alone is insufficient for a computed claim. Distinguish supplied observations, new calculations and external sources. Report failed attempts honestly. Record execution/tool-trace references where available; a self-authored research log is a summary, not independent proof of chronological decisions or blind prediction.

Complete submissions need relevant structured measurements and evidence-supported findings that address the question. The schema tests the presence/shape of records, not their scientific sufficiency. Partial work may establish bounded local findings; list the unresolved scope. Zero-launch blocked/failed reports may have no methods/models/calculations, but must explain the actual obstacle, attempted diagnostic and recovery condition; they cannot assert scientific completion. An evidenced inability to distinguish models can be complete only when the data and investigation actually justify that limitation. No credit is awarded merely for filling fields, listing hypotheses or repeating supplied observations or reference values.

The readable report must link to the same results and artifacts, explain decisions and limitations, and state what evidence could change its conclusions. Do not include internal chain-of-thought.

## Scientific scoring

Scoring uses paper_science_v3: 100 points from independent final scientific propositions. There are no points or multipliers for research process, effort, software use or reporting style. Credit requires genuine evidence for the specified scientific finding under compatible physical conditions. A contrary or unresolved result may be reported honestly but does not automatically earn points for a different target; demonstrated non-identifiability earns credit only where it is itself the scientific finding being assessed. Completion status and schema validity do not guarantee a score. Choose methods suitable for the stated physical question.

Scientific scoring uses independent final results on a 100-point scale. Structures, raw outputs, research logs and validation support those results and earn no separate points. Quantitative evidence requires matching physical definitions and real computed results, without author-value matching windows; copying a disclosed value earns no credit. Substantively equivalent supported explanations pass. Missing calculations and insufficient evidence do not become successful results by being reported as unresolved.

The research_log array may be empty, including for complete submissions. Model, execution, result and native-evidence requirements still apply; a process narrative is not a scientific prerequisite.
