# Submission guide

Submit `report/results.json` and `report/report.md`. The JSON records your research question, chosen models, actual operations, quantitative results, evidence, decisions, claims, validation and limitations. IDs and branch names are yours. Hypotheses may be added, revised or withdrawn; there is no required hypothesis count, predetermined explanation, control count or calculation matrix. A sound empirical investigation need not invent named hypotheses.

`models` must link atom-resolved identity files and explain mappings, composition, charge, spin, conditions and assumptions. New conformers, states, intermediates or finite models are allowed when physically admissible within the task's chemical scope. An input graph defines starting chemical identity; it does not prescribe geometry, a preferred endpoint or the only valid model. Justify changes of composition or state.

`operations` records the method and actual input/parameter and execution-log files, linked models, artifacts and failures. `results` contains numerical quantities with units, reference/state/condition definitions, uncertainty and extraction locators into raw products. Do not place unrelated quantities into a convenient schema field. The `other` quantity type needs a precise quantity/unit definition and receives the same physical and unit checks as named types. Every result, model, operation, claim and artifact reference must resolve uniquely.

Keep inputs, native outputs, structures, analyses and execution records under `outputs/`, `structures/`, `analysis/` or `logs/`; list their actual SHA256 in `artifacts`. Analysis of public observations is permitted, but supplied values must remain labeled public evidence rather than new calculations. Generated analyses still need reproducible code, inputs and execution records. A formatted table or self-authored log alone cannot establish that a scientific calculation happened.

`decisions` stores concise purpose/prediction, action, observed evidence and resulting continuation/revision linked to execution records. Do not submit hidden chain of thought. Self-authored timestamps do not establish preregistration; evaluator-controlled tool traces and artifact chronology support evidence provenance and earn no separate points. Report actual resources and unknown measurements honestly, and retain failed branches.

`claims` link numerical evidence to an inference and scope; `validations` state what was checked and whether it supports, limits or invalidates a claim. Evidence requirements follow the claims: a stationary-point claim requires defensible stationary-point evidence, an energetic comparison compatible state/composition/reference definitions, a spectral assignment physical state correspondence, and a causal conclusion a design capable of testing it. No particular program or named control is mandatory merely because it appears in a reference. Physically compatible findings are assessed by scientific content; evidence provenance and earned credit are distinct.

`complete` is your claim of an adequately addressed question, subject to scientific review. `partial`, `bounded_failure` and `input_blocked` preserve useful work without asserting completion. Report supported, contrary and unresolved outcomes honestly. Scientific points depend on which specified findings the evidence establishes; a contrary or unresolved result does not automatically satisfy a different scientific target. Missing work or failed convergence is not evidence of non-identifiability. Failure/blocked submissions require evidence and concrete recovery/release conditions. Format acceptance never awards scientific points. Report uncertainty and the limits of identification; no universal numerical tolerance is supplied for uncalibrated extensions.

## Scientific scoring

Scoring uses paper_science_v3 / final_results_100: 100 points from independent final scientific conclusions. Each item is pass or fail and earns its full weight or zero; insufficient or scientifically invalid support fails the affected item. There are no points or multipliers for research process, effort, software use or reporting style. Credit requires genuine evidence for the specified scientific finding under compatible physical conditions. A contrary or unresolved result may be reported honestly but does not automatically earn points for a different target; demonstrated non-identifiability earns credit only where it is itself the scientific finding being assessed. Completion status and schema validity do not guarantee a score. Choose methods independently and justify the physical comparability of the results.

## Final-results contract

Only the final scientific questions in task.md receive points (paper_science_v3 / final_results_100). Computed structures, frequencies, state assignments and native files provide evidence for their associated claims, with no independent process score. `decisions` entries document useful decisions but are not a scientific scoring gate; an empty log is allowed. Supplied facts must be distinguished from newly computed results.
