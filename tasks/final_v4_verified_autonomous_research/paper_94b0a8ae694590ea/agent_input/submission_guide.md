# Submission guide

Write `report/results.json` and `report/report.md`. The schema is an auditable record, not a prescribed research matrix. Choose meaningful object, method, hypothesis, record and quantity IDs. Hypotheses may be an empty list when the actual route does not use explicit hypotheses; their names and number are unrestricted. Describe observable definitions, units, charge/electronic state, atom correspondence and conditions precisely. Use null plus an explanation for unavailable or inapplicable numeric values, never invented zeros.

Every quantity links to objects, calculation/analysis records and artifacts. Artifacts list workspace-relative `outputs/`, `data/` or `code/` paths with actual SHA256. Preserve full native inputs/outputs, structures, measurement provenance and analysis code appropriate to your route. Referenced IDs must resolve, files must exist, and hashes must match. Evidence review must inspect those artifacts; passing JSON shape alone earns no scientific result credit. The host format validator does not by itself verify all nested IDs, artifact hashes or scientific correctness.

Decision records are concise purposes or predictions, available evidence, actual outcomes and changes of direction. Link authentic execution traces where available; your own timestamp or retrospective story cannot establish advance prediction. Do not provide hidden internal chain-of-thought.

`complete` asserts an adequately evidenced answer to the stated question, which can be supported, refuted or genuinely unresolved. It requires substantive quantitative evidence supporting the specific scientific answers; job count is not a scoring criterion. `partial` reports supported progress with remaining gaps. `bounded_failure` reports an input/resource/computation barrier and a concrete release condition. Both require failure_report; zero starts and no artifacts are valid when nothing ran, but are not a scientific pass. Uncertainty must arise from evidence, not omitted work. Claims that were revised or withdrawn remain distinguishable from supported claims.

Keep calculations at distinct conditions, on different surfaces or with different energy references distinguishable. Only make claims justified at the supplied physical scale. The investigation has no prescribed model list, named control or hypothesis count. Choose and justify your scientific methods independently. Scientific findings require physically valid evidence.

Scoring applies to independent scientific-result items on one 100-point scale. Each item earns its assigned points only when its specific scientific proposition is supported by valid evidence. Reporting an opposite, unresolved or incomplete result is allowed but does not automatically earn that item or full-question credit. Method freedom and honest reporting do not remove the result-specific scientific standard.

## Scientific scoring

The 100-point score covers only independent final scientific answers listed in the task. Each outcome is pass or fail; insufficient or scientifically invalid support fails the affected outcome. Valid equivalent explanations pass directly. Missing or scientifically invalid evidence fails the affected proposition; author-value matching windows do not apply. Given observations cannot earn credit by being copied. Native outputs, structures and validation support the conclusions without separate process points. The schema records provenance; record count, research chronology and report length are not scientific scoring gates.
