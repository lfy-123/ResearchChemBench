# Scientific objective

Determine the stable hydrogen-bonded 1:1 complexes available to neutral imidazole and neutral salicylic acid in the isolated gas phase. Independently generate and discriminate plausible intermolecular arrangements, optimize them, verify which optimized structures are local minima, and compare their consistently defined interaction energies. The research object is the set of distinct validated minima found under the stated search protocol; the requested observables are optimized structures, intermolecular contact motifs, vibrational stationary-point status, monomer/complex energies, interaction energies, and a justified final interpretation of the strongest interaction. Do not use the paper, supplementary information, or general web search.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

The public system is exactly one molecule from `data/inputs/imidazole.xyz` and one molecule from `data/inputs/salicylic_acid.xyz`, with the complete atom inventory, atom order, covalent connectivity, proton placement, total charge 0, and singlet state specified in `data/inputs/system.json`. The XYZ files are complete starting geometries (not result structures); you may generate conformers and rearrange relative orientation, but do not change covalent connectivity, protonation, stoichiometry, charge, or multiplicity. The boundary is an isolated gas-phase 1:1 complex and the two isolated monomers. Report interaction energy as E(complex) − E(imidazole) − E(salicylic_acid), with all three energies evaluated consistently. Solvent, perovskite, device, and experimental observables are outside scope. You may choose software, electronic-structure model, basis, dispersion treatment, and workflow, but disclose them and keep them consistent for every compared candidate.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Generate a finite, chemically motivated set of distinct starting arrangements that samples the imidazole N–H donor and ring-nitrogen acceptor sites against the salicylic-acid carboxyl and phenolic oxygen sites, including reasonable donor/acceptor reversals and orientations. State the generation rules and the number attempted. Optimize every advanced candidate, record convergence, and validate each claimed minimum with a vibrational calculation or another explicitly justified stationary-point test; report any imaginary modes and do not treat an unvalidated structure as a confirmed minimum. Deduplicate optimized structures using an explicitly stated structural/contact criterion while retaining candidate-level provenance. Use a consistent monomer reference protocol, record units and sign convention, and identify the lowest-energy validated interaction on that convention. Completion requires either (a) at least one converged, validated minimum and a disclosed search-coverage argument, or (b) a scientifically honest bounded-failure report describing what prevented completion and the evidence obtained. For bounded failure, do not invent energies, a validated minimum, or a strongest-candidate identity; an empty candidate list is valid when no candidate was advanced. Stop when additional chemically distinct starting arrangements no longer produce a new validated minimum under your stated generation rules, or when a declared computational/resource limit is reached; in either case report the stopping rule, every attempted candidate (including discarded candidates), and remaining coverage limitations. Do not claim exhaustive discovery beyond the explored space.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` conforming to `submission_schema.json`. It must contain the chosen method and settings, candidate-level identities and validation context, monomer and complex energies with units, interaction energies, deduplication/coverage/stopping records, and a conclusion that distinguishes computed evidence from limitations. A bounded-failure branch is acceptable only when the candidate and coverage fields truthfully document the failed or partial investigation. Include enough geometry/contact information to make each candidate unambiguous; do not submit paper source files or copied paper text.
