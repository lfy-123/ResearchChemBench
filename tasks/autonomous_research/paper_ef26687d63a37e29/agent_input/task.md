# Scientific objective

Using three fixed molecular systems, determine whether computed structure, contact geometry, charge redistribution, and electrostatic-potential features provide evidence for interactions between the phosphorus-containing center and an epoxide-like oxygen or carboxylate/alkoxide chain-end environment. Report optimized structures, stationary-point validation, O···P/O···αH/O···βH distances where defined by the supplied atom identities, ADCH charge comparisons, MEP analysis, and a conclusion limited to what these isolated-molecule calculations establish. Do not assume an author mechanism or a preferred interaction pattern.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

The files `data/inputs/Et2N3P.xyz`, `Et2N3P_PO.xyz`, and `Et2N3P_PA.xyz` are atom-indexed starting structures in Å for neutral singlet `(Et2N)3P`, `(Et2N)3P-PO`, and `(Et2N)3P-PA`. PO is the ring-opened propylene-oxide adduct; PA is the phthalic-anhydride-derived acylphosphonium/carboxylate zwitterion. The PA identity is `CCN(CC)[P+](C(=O)c1ccccc1C(=O)[O-])(N(CC)CC)N(CC)CC`, formula C20H34N3O3P. PA rows 1–51 come from the printed SI table; aromatic C52–C57 and H58–H61 were generated from the defined molecular graph because the printed table omitted that fragment. They are starting coordinates, not an author optimized result. `data/inputs/structure_provenance.json` records the distinction. No additional species, counterion, solvent or polymer is part of the model.

For these N-ethyl substituents, αH denotes an N–CH2 hydrogen and βH an N–CH2–CH3 terminal hydrogen; the structure has P–N–C connectivity, not a direct P–C bond to an ethyl group. Derive and report this atom mapping from the inputs. Measure O···P, O···αH and O···βH contacts for the PO chain-end oxygen and the PA carboxylate group, retaining explicit oxygen and hydrogen indices. Report P and N-ethyl hydrogen ADCH charges, and analyze a signed electrostatic-potential surface. Distinguish electrostatic potential from electron density. All systems are isolated ground-state molecules. The paper and SI are unavailable during evaluation; no general web or literature search is allowed. Select and disclose software, functional, basis, dispersion, convergence and analysis settings. Do not infer targets from the supplied coordinates.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Optimize all three structures and validate each final structure as a minimum by frequency analysis or a clearly justified equivalent. Define atom indices before measuring contacts and retain them per species. Use one consistent charge-analysis provenance per structure and distinguish direct observations from interpretation. Completion requires one converged final structure and one validation analysis for every species, or a bounded-failure report with exact failed species, cause, attempted remedies, and missing observables. Stop when this endpoint is met or when a documented bounded failure remains after reasonable method/convergence adjustments; state coverage and limitations, including sensitivity to conformer or method choices. Formulate any mechanistic explanation from your calculations and discriminate at least one plausible alternative interpretation (for example, electrostatics without a specific contact, or geometry/charge changes too method-sensitive to support a strong claim).

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` following `submission_schema.json`, plus `report/structures/` files and `report/figures/` or numerical MEP data. Include method provenance, per-species status, frequency validation, explicit atom-indexed distances and charges, MEP observations, an independently reasoned final conclusion, an alternative interpretation, and limitations. Do not claim a literature result or report unsupported values.
