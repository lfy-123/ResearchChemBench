# Public system definition

`system.json` is the complete identity contract. Build four separate neutral singlet molecules from the mapped connectivity and impose the listed CIP configurations at mapped atoms 1 and 2. The azide is represented by the charge-separated resonance form N=N+=N−; the whole molecule is neutral. Generate 3D coordinates and conformers independently. The comparison endpoint is the lowest validated conformer standard Gibbs free energy of each stereoisomer in water at 298.15 K under one consistently documented model. No database lookup is required.
