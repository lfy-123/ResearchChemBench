# Scientific objective

Investigate the isolated, single-collision reaction of ground-state SiN radical with isoprene and determine which neutral singlet SiNC5H7 product structures are computationally plausible after atomic-hydrogen loss. Generate and discriminate plausible product connectivities and mechanistic explanations independently. Report optimized product structures, relative 0 K reaction energies, and any intermediates or transition states used to support the conclusion. The measured endpoint is each candidate's reaction energy relative to separated SiN + isoprene, compared with the experimentally reported exoergicity window provided in `data/inputs/reactants.json`.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

The experimental channel reaction-energy window is −162 ± 27 kJ/mol; the 25 ± 1 kJ/mol collision energy is a different input. Report ΔE0 = E0(product) + E0(H) − E0(SiN) − E0(isoprene), using consistent 0 K electronic-plus-ZPE energies, with neutral doublet atomic H. Report H energy with the reactant/component energies. Two distinct validated product candidates are required for complete results. TS/intermediate calculations are optional, but any claimed TS must have the stated frequency and connectivity evidence.

Use `data/inputs/reactants.json`. It uniquely specifies neutral SiN radical `[Si]#N` (doublet) and neutral isoprene `CC(=C)C=C` (singlet), the neutral singlet product formula SiNC5H7, atomic-H loss, and the single-collision collision-energy boundary. Do not use the paper, SI, general web, or hidden evaluator files. You may generate 3D structures and choose computational methods, but state software, model chemistry, conformer treatment, and energy convention. The scored object is independently discovered product connectivity and its reaction energetics; do not assume a product structure from an internal label.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Define a finite candidate-generation procedure covering distinct SiNC5H7 connectivities and relevant conformers, and state how duplicate graphs are removed. Propose multiple plausible mechanistic hypotheses when more than one exists, then discriminate them with calculations. For every advanced candidate retain an unambiguous ID, structure file/SMILES, charge and multiplicity, and provenance. Optimize candidates and verify minima with a frequency calculation or a justified equivalent; report imaginary-frequency evidence. Compute the reaction energy against separately computed reactant energies using a stated consistent convention and units. If a transition state or intermediate is claimed, report its structure, frequency signature, and connectivity evidence (IRC or a scientifically justified alternative). Compare every validated candidate's energy with the experimentally reported exoergicity window, and distinguish energetic compatibility from proof of experimental branching. Use the supplied experimental observation for comparison; calculate the candidate energies independently. Completion requires at least two independently generated, validated product candidates plus a reproducible comparison. If this is not achieved, submit a failure/partial report documenting completed candidates, explored generation space and the specific missing validations.

A complete outcome requires the requested scientific results and their validation evidence. If only part succeeds, use the existing failure/partial pathway and submit completed results plus the specific missing calculations and diagnostics; do not fabricate values. Extra exploratory attempts are allowed and do not invalidate completed main results. General limitations or stopping statements are optional, not scored deliverables.
