# Scientific objective

Determine whether the neutral E and Z stereoisomers of 1,2-bis(tetrazol-5-yl)ethylene (H2bte) have distinct optimized electronic structures and noncovalent-interaction descriptors, and whether those differences are consistent with the authors' qualitative hypothesis that molecular electronic structure and intermolecular interactions help explain stability and mechanical-sensitivity differences. Independently plan calculations; do not assume the authors' software or model chemistry. Report ESP minimum and maximum for each named isomer in kcal mol−1, NCI interaction features, and LOL-p delocalization features.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use `data/inputs/system_manifest.json`, `e_h2bte.xyz`, and `z_h2bte.xyz`. Both structures are neutral singlets with formula C4H4N8 and explicitly labeled E/Z central-alkene stereochemistry; XYZ coordinates are starting guesses and must not be treated as results. The scope has two distinct levels: optimized isolated-molecule ESP/LOL-π and frozen crystal-derived intermolecular NCI. Use the supplied CCDC1872388/2474069 CIFs and `data/inputs/crystal_nci_protocol.json` to reconstruct complete molecules and all independent first-shell pairs. Synthesis, irradiation and measured bulk sensitivity are interpretation context, not computed observables. Do not use the paper, SI, general web, or hidden reference values. Do not report an unlabeled “best” structure: every result must retain `E-H2bte` or `Z-H2bte` identity.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

For each named isomer, generate at least one optimized structure from the supplied connectivity and preserve charge/multiplicity. The submission must contain exactly two isomer records, in the manifest order `E-H2bte`, then `Z-H2bte`, with each record's `id` explicitly naming the object. Verify atom count, connectivity, E/Z identity, optimization convergence, and (where feasible) absence of imaginary frequencies; disclose any alternative validation and failures. Use one consistently defined electron-density/ESP surface protocol for both isomers and record grid/surface settings, units, and software. Produce an NCI analysis or a scientifically justified equivalent that distinguishes attractive hydrogen-bond-like regions from dispersion/π-stacking-like regions, and a like-for-like LOL-π/delocalization assessment if available. Compare the two isomers only after individual validation. Completion requires either validated results for both isomers or a bounded-failure report naming the missing calculation, evidence, and what conclusion remains supportable. Stop when both isomers have passed the stated validation and all requested observables are reported, or when a reproducible computational/software/data limitation prevents that; do not silently substitute unvalidated values.

# Deliverables

Write `report/results.json` conforming to the submission schema. Include methods, per-isomer structure and validation records, ESP extrema, NCI and LOL-π observations, comparison, limitations, and a concise conclusion. Include enough provenance (input file, charge, multiplicity, method, solvent, settings, software, and output filenames or hashes) for another researcher to reproduce the investigation. Preserve CIF symmetry operations, translations, molecular components, pair multiplicities and geometry hashes. Follow the matched gas/PCM density and grid-convergence protocol supplied above; its finite-cluster choices are explicit benchmark conventions, not assumed original unpublished author input. Include all crystallographically independent Z molecules and normalize summaries per central molecule. Do not count more plotted Z pairs as stronger physical interaction. Keep molecular ESP/LOL and crystal NCI evidence separate.
