# Scientific objective

Determine, by an independently designed periodic electronic-structure calculation, whether the supplied compound-14 crystal model supports the authors' qualitative interpretation of bonding and electron distribution. Report the relaxed crystal and characterize total/projected DOS and ELF (or an explicitly justified equivalent real-space localization analysis). The authors' qualitative route is disclosed only as context: they used DFT electronic-structure analysis to examine bonding and polarity; independently plan calculations that test this interpretation. Do not assume any paper numerical result.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

`data/inputs/compound14.cif` is the complete periodic starting model: LuFe(SO4)3(H2O), formula LuFeS3O13, R3c (No. 161), a=b=8.7636 Å, c=21.9397 Å, α=β=90°, γ=120°, Z=6, with the unique asymmetric-unit sites Lu1, Fe1, S1 and O1–O5. Fractional coordinates and symmetry operations are given in the CIF. Use neutral-crystal stoichiometry with Lu(III), Fe(III), sulfate S(VI), oxide/water oxygen, and an overall singlet/triplet/etc. treatment only if justified by the periodic model; state charge, spin treatment, pseudopotentials, k-point sampling, smearing and all other choices. The target is the periodic solid at its electronic ground state within the chosen reproducible model. The supplied experimental cell is a structural reference, not a scored electronic answer.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Choose and document a reproducible periodic method. Relax internal coordinates and, if cell relaxation is performed, report the resulting cell and whether symmetry was constrained. Demonstrate numerical convergence for the reported calculation and retain logs or equivalent evidence. Validate the relaxed structure by reporting cell metrics, composition, symmetry handling and a coordinate comparison or displacement summary against the supplied starting model. Compute total and element/orbital-resolved DOS on a stated energy reference and report the energy range and resolution. Compute ELF or a clearly defined equivalent localization field and provide a rendered map or quantitative descriptors tied to named regions/atoms. Interpret separately: (i) deep-valence O contribution, (ii) intermediate sulfate S/O contribution, (iii) upper-valence Fe/O overlap, (iv) Lu contribution, and (v) O-centered versus Lu-centered localization and inversion/polarity. Completion requires all required artifacts, reproducibility metadata, convergence evidence and an evidence-backed conclusion; a failed or partial calculation is acceptable only when the report states what was completed, why it stopped, and which requested observables remain unsupported. Stop when the chosen convergence checks are stable and the DOS/ELF analyses are complete, or when a documented resource/software limitation prevents this; do not claim unsupported agreement.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` plus referenced files under `report/`. The JSON must contain `status`, `method`, `structure`, `dos`, `elf`, `validation`, and `conclusion`; each must identify the calculation and its evidence paths. Include `status` as `complete` or `partial`, and for partial work include a limitation. `dos` must include named feature assessments and a data artifact path; `elf` must include named-region assessments and a map/data artifact path. The conclusion must state whether the calculation supports the bonding and noncentrosymmetric-electron-distribution interpretation, with scope and uncertainty. Include coordinate/cell output, DOS data/plot, ELF data/plot, and logs or convergence evidence.
