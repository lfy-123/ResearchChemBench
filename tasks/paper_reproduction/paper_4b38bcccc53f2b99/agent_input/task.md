# Scientific objective

Independently compute the one-electron Pr(IV)/Pr(III) oxidation potential of the uniquely identified potassium-containing homoleptic complex K[Pr(NP(tBu)2(pyrrolidinyl))4], conventionally called 1-KPr(NPC2), on a Fc/Fc+ scale in THF. The authors' qualitative hypothesis to test is that the potassium counterion remains associated during the oxidation and that this retained-counterion model is the chemically relevant comparison. Do not assume that hypothesis is correct: calculate and evaluate it. The measured quantity is the adiabatic Pr4+/Pr3+ potential in volts versus Fc/Fc+; the experimental boundary for discussion is the irreversible 1-KPr(NPC2) CV wave in THF.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use `data/inputs/structure_record.json` and the controlled Figshare record DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.30338485, selecting the entry labelled `1-KPr(NPC2)`. This is the only structure source permitted for the starting coordinates. The formula is K[Pr(NP(tBu)2(pyrrolidinyl))4], with four monoanionic NPC2 ligands, neutral total charge, Pr(III), and singlet multiplicity for the supplied starting state. Define the oxidized state from the same connectivity with one electron removed and K+ retained; report and justify its charge and multiplicity. The thermodynamic model is molecular gas phase for optimization/frequencies and implicit THF for solution energies, with Fc/Fc+ as the reference. Explicit solvent, crystal packing, counterion exchange with an unmodelled species, and reproduction of the voltammogram are outside scope. The published experimental boundary is Epa = −0.60 V and Epc = −1.47 V versus Fc/Fc+ for the irreversible Pr4+/Pr3+ wave in THF; it is only a comparison boundary, not the requested theoretical target.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Plan and execute a defensible independent electronic-structure workflow. Optimize the supplied Pr(III) structure and the oxidized Pr(IV) structure without constraining chemically meaningful degrees of freedom; perform a stationary-point test (preferably frequencies) and report imaginary modes if present. Compute the THF-solvated energy and thermal/ZPE terms needed for an adiabatic oxidation free-energy difference, calibrate or reference it to Fc/Fc+, and state all conventions, units, software, model chemistry, convergence settings and charge/spin choices. Validate atom counts/connectivity after retrieval, retain retrieval metadata and SHA-256, and provide enough intermediate quantities to audit the potential. The calculation is complete when both oxidation states have converged geometries or a clearly documented bounded failure, the stationary-point status is recorded, the reference calculation is documented, and a potential or scientifically justified inability to obtain one is reported. Stop after these required states and one justified reference calculation are complete; if resources prevent completion, stop and submit the bounded-failure branch with completed evidence, attempted states and the limiting cause rather than inventing a number.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` conforming to `submission_schema.json`. Include the retrieved coordinate hash, identity and charge/spin audit, optimization and frequency evidence for each state, thermochemical/electronic terms, reference treatment, computed potential when available, comparison to the experimental boundary, uncertainty/limitation statement, and a concise conclusion about whether the retained-K hypothesis is supported within the chosen model. A bounded failure is acceptable only with the required failure details and no fabricated result.
