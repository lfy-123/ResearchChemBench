# Scientific objective

Independently test the authors' qualitative hypothesis that neutral HPY 10a has a nearly coplanar hydrazone-linked heteroaromatic core while its R1/R2/R3 substituent groups are twisted out of that core plane. Build the uniquely identified molecule in `data/inputs/compound_identity.json`, obtain a defensible ground-state structure in a dichloromethane continuum, and report the three SI Table S4 dihedrals (θ1, θ2, θ3) and the ground-state HOMO/LUMO energies. The target is the computed molecular minimum, not an experimental crystal structure or a spectrum.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

The only chemical input is HPY 10a: ethyl (E)-6-(2-((3-(ethoxycarbonyl)-4,5-diphenyl-1H-pyrrol-2-yl)methylene)hydrazineyl)-5-isocyano-2-methyl-4-phenylnicotinate, formula C33H30N6O4, charge 0, multiplicity 1, with the E hydrazone geometry and 1H pyrrole tautomer stated in the identity file. The physical boundary is one isolated neutral molecule with implicit dichloromethane solvation. You may generate conformers and choose a computational method, but must report method, basis, dispersion, solvent model, convergence, and all starting/conformer coverage. Define θ1, θ2 and θ3 by explicit four-atom selections and explain how those selections correspond to the R1/R2/R3 substituent-to-pyrrole/pyridine torsions; do not rely on an internal paper atom label.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Generate and deduplicate a finite set of chemically distinct starting conformers, optimize candidates, and advance only converged structures with chemically valid connectivity and the requested charge/multiplicity. Validate the selected structure with a frequency calculation or a scientifically justified alternative that distinguishes a local minimum from a saddle point. Report whether imaginary modes remain, the lowest frequency if available, and how conformer selection was made. Calculate θ1–θ3 and HOMO/LUMO for the selected validated structure, with units and sign conventions. The investigation is complete when at least one validated minimum has been obtained, all five observables and their atom mappings are reported, and the method/conformer coverage and limitations are documented. Stop after the documented conformer-generation strategy has been exhausted and no additional chemically distinct low-energy starting conformer is identified; do not claim a global minimum without coverage evidence.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` plus any supporting files named there. For a successful investigation, use status=`completed` and include the selected structure (SMILES or MOL/SDF path), method and convergence, conformer/optimization summary, frequency validation, explicitly named dihedral records `theta1`, `theta2`, and `theta3` (each with four atom indices and degrees), HOMO and LUMO in eV, and a conclusion about the qualitative geometry claim. If the bounded investigation cannot obtain a validated minimum, use status=`bounded_failure` and report the failure reason, attempted work, and limitations without fabricating observables or structures. Do not include paper/SI files or hidden reference values.
