# Scientific objective

The authors qualitatively use this compound as a calibration case for whether a quantum-chemical geometry model describes helicene strain; independently plan and execute calculations that test that calibration. The scored observable is the arithmetic mean, in degrees, of five individual inner-rim dihedrals that you identify unambiguously by four atom indices in the supplied ordering (symmetry-equivalent sites may be averaged only after being listed individually).

The task does not disclose the authors' numerical answer, software, model chemistry, convergence settings, or ordered protocol. You may use any computational chemistry software and defensible model, but must state it. The target state is a stationary equilibrium structure reached from the supplied geometry. A calculation is complete when an optimized structure, a stationary-point/minimum check, all five named dihedrals with atom indices and convention, their arithmetic mean, and uncertainty/limitations are reported. Stop after the chosen route has converged and the minimum check and at least one independent plausibility check are complete; if this cannot be achieved, report bounded failure with diagnostics rather than inventing a result.
