# Scientific objective

Determine, by an independently planned computational investigation, how the neutral singlet pyridinium bromide catalyst F interacts with four explicitly supplied neutral singlet molecules: phenolic acceptor 2g, its glycoside 3g, alcoholic acceptor 2w, and its glycoside 3w. Report the interaction energy ΔE(X–F)=E(X–F)−E(X)−E(F) in kcal/mol for each named pair and use the four values to test the qualitative author hypothesis that catalyst organization by a phenolic acceptor is more favorable than by an alcohol acceptor and that product binding can inhibit turnover. The author hypothesis is supplied only as a qualitative hypothesis to test; do not assume its direction is correct.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

The files `data/inputs/2g.xyz`, `2w.xyz`, `3g.xyz`, `3w.xyz`, and `F.xyz` are the complete Cartesian coordinates in Å for the named molecules, each with charge 0 and spin multiplicity 1. The XYZ comment line identifies the molecule. Use the atom identities and coordinates exactly as supplied; do not alter protonation, connectivity, stereochemistry, charge, or multiplicity. The four complexes to investigate are exactly 2g-F, 3g-F, 2w-F, and 3w-F, each a noncovalent 1:1 complex. The physical boundary is gas-phase molecular quantum chemistry with an optional implicit toluene model chosen and justified by the Agent; no explicit solvent, counterion beyond the bromine atom present in F, reaction transition state, or kinetic model is requested. The measured quantity is the electronic interaction energy assembled from consistently computed complex and monomer energies. The paper, SI, general web, and hidden reference values are unavailable during the task.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Plan and execute a defensible search over starting arrangements for each of the four complexes. Define how arrangements are generated, how duplicate structures are removed, and which optimized stationary points are advanced to final energy evaluation. For every advanced structure, report an optimization/frequency or equivalent stationary-point validation outcome, its charge and multiplicity, and enough identity information to distinguish it from other arrangements. A valid minimum must have no imaginary frequency when frequencies are computed, or the Agent must explicitly justify an alternative validation and mark the result as limited. Compute isolated-monomer energies and complex energies with a consistent energy convention and show the arithmetic for all four ΔE values. Completion requires either (a) at least one validated minimum and a final interaction energy for every named pair, or (b) a bounded-failure report identifying the exact pair(s), attempted search coverage, failed validation, and the evidence needed to complete them. Stop when each pair has a validated advanced structure and the energy assembly is complete, or when additional attempts no longer produce a validated structure under the stated computational resources; report the stopping reason and search coverage. Do not claim a global minimum or a kinetic/product-inhibition mechanism from interaction energies alone.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` conforming to `submission_schema.json`. It must contain the completion status, per-pair identities and validation context, monomer/complex energies or an explicit bounded-failure branch, four interaction energies when complete, search coverage, and a conclusion that addresses the qualitative hypothesis and limitations. Include units and computational method details used by the Agent, but do not omit unsuccessful attempts.
