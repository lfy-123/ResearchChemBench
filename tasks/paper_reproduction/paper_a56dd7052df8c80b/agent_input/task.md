# Scientific objective

Independently test the authors' qualitative hypothesis that interaction of propargyl alcohol 1a with metavanadate can activate its hydroxyl group, and compare the Gibbs free-energy barrier for O-H cleavage in the metavanadate-assisted pathway with the barrier for a CO2-prebound metavanadate alternative. The research objects are the two pathway-specific transition states and their defined reactant/product reference states; report barriers in kcal/mol. The authors' proposed route is supplied only as a qualitative hypothesis: examine an alcohol-O/vanadium coordination pathway and compare it against a pathway in which CO2 is associated with metavanadate before alcohol binding. Do not treat that hypothesis as established.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use `data/inputs/reactants.smi` and `data/inputs/system.json`. They uniquely specify neutral singlet 2-methylbut-3-yn-2-ol (1a), singlet monomeric [VO3]−, and singlet CO2, including connectivity and charge. Construct 3-D structures and any complexes yourself. The assisted endpoint is the O-bound alcohol/metavanadate complex through O-H cleavage to an alkoxy-metavanadate product; the alternative endpoint is the CO2-preassociated metavanadate/alcohol complex through the analogous O-H cleavage. The boundary is an isolated molecular-cluster model; state all model, solvation, standard-state and conformer assumptions. The paper, SI and general web are unavailable. Do not report a result as complete if identity, charge, multiplicity or atom mapping was guessed.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Choose and justify an electronic-structure and thermochemical protocol. Generate multiple chemically distinct starting arrangements for each endpoint, deduplicate optimized structures by connectivity and a stated geometry criterion, and advance only candidates that preserve the intended O-H cleavage connectivity. Optimize minima and first-order saddle points, characterize frequencies (zero imaginary modes for minima and exactly one for each claimed transition state), and verify the imaginary mode involves O-H cleavage rather than an unrelated motion. Compute both activation free energies from explicitly named reference states and use one consistent convention. Completion requires at least one validated saddle point for each pathway, an auditable energy table, and a comparison/conclusion supported by those states. Stop when both pathways have validated stationary points and additional starting arrangements no longer produce a materially distinct lower-barrier candidate under your stated search protocol; otherwise report bounded failure, coverage and the limitation that the target was not established. Record software, versions, input files, convergence, frequencies, mapping and uncertainty.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` conforming to `submission_schema.json`. Include pathway-specific structures or structure-file paths, stationary-point validation, barrier values, method provenance, candidate coverage, and a final mechanistic conclusion. A bounded-failure branch is allowed, but it must identify which pathway or validation step failed and still report the attempted coverage and limitation.
