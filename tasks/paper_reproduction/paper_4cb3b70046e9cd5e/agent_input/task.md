# Scientific objective

Determine the Gibbs free-energy barriers for the rate-controlling nucleophilic ring-opening step in the bulk reaction of styrene oxide with 2,6-dimethylaniline (DMeA) in ethanol, comparing aminolysis and alcoholysis. The authors qualitatively propose proton activation of the epoxide followed by nucleophilic attack; independently plan calculations that test this proposed route. Report ΔG‡ for each channel, ΔΔG‡ = ΔG‡(aminolysis) − ΔG‡(alcoholysis), the lower-barrier channel, and the defensible chemoselectivity interpretation.

Optimize candidates and verify every accepted IM1 has no imaginary frequency and every accepted TS has exactly one imaginary frequency whose displacement follows proton-assisted epoxide opening and formation of the channel-defining bond.
