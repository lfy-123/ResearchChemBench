# Scientific objective

Determine whether the supplied computational model of the clathriamine A dication supports an absolute-configuration assignment by independently calculating solution ECD for the public (1R,2S,3R,4S) model and comparing it with the measured natural-product trace. Report predicted wavelength-dependent ECD/rotatory-strength features and an assignment of the isolated material, or a scientifically justified inconclusive result.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

The research object is the C22H22Br2N8 clathriamine A connectivity represented by each supplied XYZ file, with molecular charge +2 and singlet multiplicity. `data/inputs/conformer_2.xyz` and `conformer_3.xyz` are two Cartesian starting geometries of the (1R,2S,3R,4S) stereoisomer, supplied with 54 atoms for C22H22Br2N8, charge +2 and singlet multiplicity; atom ordering is the file ordering. `data/inputs/structure_provenance.json` records that these are unmodified original paper-SI S10 (PDF page 12) coordinate recoveries. The solvent boundary is methanol. `experimental_ecd_sign_anchors.json` contains measured-spectrum sign anchors at named wavelengths in nm and is the only experimental comparison data. You may optimize, reorient, generate additional conformers, compute excited states and construct broadened spectra. Do not use the paper, SI, general web, or hidden reference data. The scored endpoint is the predicted ECD sign pattern/broad shape and the resulting absolute-configuration assignment, not connectivity, biological activity, or a claim of mechanistic proof.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

For each public geometry, verify that the charge, multiplicity, atom count, connectivity and stereochemical identity are preserved; report any failed or discarded calculation. Independently choose a defensible electronic-structure and solvent treatment, optimize or otherwise validate the geometries, and calculate sufficient excited states to cover the 200–300 nm region. If more than the two supplied conformers are used, generate candidates by a stated reproducible rule, deduplicate them with a stated structural criterion, and retain each advanced candidate with its identity, energy/population and validation status. Construct a population-weighted ECD prediction and compare it with all four supplied experimental sign anchors plus the broad spectral pattern. A completed investigation must include at least one converged/validated prediction for each retained candidate and a reasoned configuration assignment or an explicitly bounded inconclusive outcome. A failed or compute-bounded investigation may instead report the candidates and checks completed, the failed or unavailable calculations, and the untested comparison without fabricated spectra or assignment. Stop when the stated wavelength coverage is achieved, all advanced candidates have been validated, and adding a new candidate or method sensitivity test would not change the reported assignment; otherwise state the limitation and stop at the available compute boundary.

# Deliverables

Write `report/results.json` conforming to `submission_schema.json`. Include the calculation route, public-object identity checks, candidate records, predicted spectral features, comparison evidence for each anchor, assignment/inconclusive status, uncertainty and stopping rationale. Include enough numerical or tabular output to reproduce the reported comparison and identify every candidate-specific validation artifact. Do not report unsupported precision.
