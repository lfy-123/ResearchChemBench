# Scientific objective

Determine computationally whether the periodic high-pressure Cd3(C3N6) crystal model is structurally stable after independent electronic-structure relaxation and whether its relaxed geometry and chemical-bonding observables support the authors' qualitative hypothesis that Cd-N covalency constrains a distorted melaminate anion. You may test the proposed catalyst-free solid-state explanation, but the winning numerical values and any result-bearing structure are not supplied.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use only `data/inputs/cd3c3n6_experimental_47p7GPa.cif` and `data/inputs/structure_manifest.json`. They define neutral periodic Cd3(C3N6), R3c (No. 161), hexagonal cell a=b=11.5351 Å, c=5.188 Å, angles 90°,90°,120°, Z=6, pressure 47.7 GPa, and the labelled Cd1, C1, N1 and N2 18b asymmetric-unit sites. Reconstruct the full cell with standard R3c symmetry; preserve composition, charge and site labels. The scored structural objects are the three inequivalent anion C-N classes. You may call them x, y and z only after giving a neutral reproducible atom-label or graph-based mapping; any permutation of those names is acceptable. Also report the Cd-N contacts identified in the relaxed structure. The scored observables are C-N distances, a quantitative distortion descriptor, Cd-N Wiberg-Mayer bond orders or a chemically equivalent bond-index analysis, and C-N ICOBI contributions or a clearly justified alternative.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Independently choose and document model chemistry, numerical settings, pressure/cell treatment, charge and spin treatment, and the ordered calculation protocol. Relax the periodic structure, verify force/energy/stress convergence, and report whether the final structure retains the intended composition and crystallographic identity. Generate and deduplicate the three C-N classes by explicit atom labels or a reproducible symmetry/graph selector; report all members or the multiplicity-weighted class means. Validate the reported Cd-N contacts individually and state the bond-index method and basis/projection diagnostics. Compute a distortion metric that distinguishes planar from out-of-plane melaminate geometry and explain its coordinate definition. The calculation is complete only when the relaxed structure, convergence evidence, class mapping, bond-index evidence and an interpretation with uncertainty/sensitivity notes are all present. Stop after one converged primary calculation plus at least one justified sensitivity or validation check; if convergence or bonding projection fails, stop with a bounded limitation report that identifies the failed stage, attempted remedies and the remaining evidence.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` conforming to `submission_schema.json`. For a complete outcome include the selected computational route, convergence and identity checks, explicit C-N and Cd-N object mappings, numerical observables with units, validation evidence, limitations, and a final conclusion about whether the results support the qualitative covalency/distortion hypothesis. If a required stage cannot be completed, use the bounded-failure branch and report the failed stage, attempted remedies, and evidence needed to resume; do not fabricate success-only observables. Do not cite or reproduce the paper or SI as an execution substitute.
