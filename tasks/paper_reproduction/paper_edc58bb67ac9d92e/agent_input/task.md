# Scientific objective

Test the paper’s qualitative proposal that N-benzoyl carbazole coordinates to BBr3, undergoes bromide transfer involving a second BBr3, then intramolecular electrophilic C–H borylation and deprotonation. Independently plan and execute calculations to obtain the validated free-energy profile in dichloroethane, the activation free energy for the C–H borylation transition state, the overall barrier from adduct A plus the second BBr3 to the highest subsequent validated transition state, and the rate-determining step. Keep the full profile referenced to separated substrate plus two BBr3 molecules. Do not assume the paper’s numerical values or optimized geometries.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use `data/inputs/system.json` as the complete system definition. It gives unique connectivity by SMILES, neutral singlet charge state, reagent stoichiometry, solvent identity and the separated reference state. The modeled boundary ends at the borylation intermediate after deprotonation; oxidation/deprotection chemistry is excluded. Report 298 K Gibbs free energies in kcal/mol and define every state by submitted structure, charge/multiplicity and role in the sequence. You may generate conformers and starting geometries, but must disclose how.

For answer-neutral state identity, use these structural/chemical definitions: A is the N-benzoyl-carbazole carbonyl-O Lewis adduct to one BBr3; B is the post-bromide-transfer borenium species formed with the second BBr3 present; C is the Wheland (arenium) intermediate after intramolecular electrophilic attack at carbazole C1; D is the deprotonated C1-borylation intermediate. TS A-B and TS B-C are the connecting saddle points for those named neighboring states. These definitions do not specify a geometry, conformer, energy, or preferred search candidate.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Generate and deduplicate a finite set of candidate geometries for each minimum and transition state in the stated sequence. Advance candidates only when their connectivity and elemental composition match the assigned state. Validate each minimum with a frequency calculation showing zero imaginary frequencies; validate each transition state with exactly one imaginary frequency and an IRC (or a chemically equivalent bidirectional path-following validation) connecting the two named neighboring minima. Report failed or unresolved searches explicitly. The calculation is complete when every state needed for the profile has either a validated result or a documented bounded failure, all relative free energies use one stated reference and consistent thermal/solvent treatment, and no unvalidated state is used in the final barrier. Stop after the planned candidate set has been exhausted and further searches no longer produce a distinct validated state, or earlier with a scientifically justified limitation.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` conforming to `submission_schema.json`. In a completed submission include the calculation method, software, solvent model, state records with identity and validation evidence, relative Gibbs free energies, the TS B–C activation free energy, the overall barrier, rate-determining-step claim, candidate coverage and limitations. Include enough provenance (input geometry hashes or files and job references) for an evaluator to reproduce the reported arithmetic. A bounded-failure branch is allowed when a required state cannot be located, but it must identify the missing state, attempted searches and the resulting limitation; in that branch do not invent energies, barriers, structures, or a rate-determining-step conclusion for the missing/unvalidated portion. Numeric barriers and the rate-determining-step conclusion are required only for a completed profile.
