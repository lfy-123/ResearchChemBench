# Scientific objective

Determine, by independent quantum-chemical calculation, whether the three supplied species—neutral singlet `(Et2N)3P`, neutral singlet `(Et2N)3P-PO`, and neutral singlet `(Et2N)3P-PA`—show the phosphonium/oxygen and phosphonium/chain-end interactions relevant to the paper's proposed mechanism. In reproduction mode, the authors' qualitative hypothesis is that an onium center can activate an epoxide and stabilize an alkoxide-like chain end through noncovalent contacts; test that hypothesis without assuming any numerical result. Report optimized structures, stationary-point validation, O···P/O···αH/O···βH distances where the named atoms exist, ADCH charge comparisons, and an interpretable MEP analysis.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

The files `data/inputs/Et2N3P.xyz`, `Et2N3P_PO.xyz`, and `Et2N3P_PA.xyz` are atom-indexed starting structures in Å for neutral singlet `(Et2N)3P`, `(Et2N)3P-PO`, and `(Et2N)3P-PA`. PO is the ring-opened propylene-oxide adduct; PA is the phthalic-anhydride-derived acylphosphonium/carboxylate zwitterion. The PA identity is `CCN(CC)[P+](C(=O)c1ccccc1C(=O)[O-])(N(CC)CC)N(CC)CC`, formula C20H34N3O3P. PA rows 1–51 come from the printed SI table; aromatic C52–C57 and H58–H61 were generated from the defined molecular graph because the printed table omitted that fragment. They are starting coordinates, not an author optimized result. `data/inputs/structure_provenance.json` records the distinction. No additional species, counterion, solvent or polymer is part of the model.

For these N-ethyl substituents, αH denotes an N–CH2 hydrogen and βH an N–CH2–CH3 terminal hydrogen; the structure has P–N–C connectivity, not a direct P–C bond to an ethyl group. Derive and report this atom mapping from the inputs. Measure O···P, O···αH and O···βH contacts for the PO chain-end oxygen and the PA carboxylate group, retaining explicit oxygen and hydrogen indices. Report P and N-ethyl hydrogen ADCH charges, and analyze a signed electrostatic-potential surface. Distinguish electrostatic potential from electron density. All systems are isolated ground-state molecules. The paper and SI are unavailable during evaluation; no general web or literature search is allowed. Select and disclose software, functional, basis, dispersion, convergence and analysis settings. Do not infer targets from the supplied coordinates.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Independently optimize all three supplied structures and perform a frequency or equivalent stationary-point check. A completed calculation requires either (a) all three structures optimized and validated as minima, or (b) a bounded-failure report identifying the exact failed species, cause, attempted remedy, and the observables that remain unavailable. Measure each requested contact using explicit atom indices and preserve those indices in the report. Extract charges from the same validated wavefunction used for the charge analysis; do not mix atom order between structures. Produce an MEP visualization or numerical surface description and explain whether it supports, weakens, or does not resolve the qualitative hypothesis. Stop after each species has one converged final structure plus one validation analysis, or after a documented bounded failure after reasonable method/convergence adjustments; report conformational or method limitations rather than silently selecting a result.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` following `submission_schema.json`, plus `report/structures/` files and `report/figures/` or numerical MEP data. The JSON must include method provenance, per-species status, frequency validation, atom-indexed distances and charges, MEP observations, a final mechanistic conclusion, and limitations. Every successful or bounded-failure branch must identify what was actually computed; invented values are not acceptable.
