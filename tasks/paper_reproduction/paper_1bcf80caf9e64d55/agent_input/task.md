# Scientific objective

Independently plan and execute a computational investigation of the neutral, singlet PEt3-catalysed reaction of substrate 1a with 4-fluorophenol (2a), testing the authors' qualitative hypothesis that phosphine addition to the alkyne initiates a zwitterion-mediated alkyne–ester stitching cascade. Determine a connected catalytic pathway to the specified cyclopentenone endpoint, validate every reported minimum and transition state, and report relative Gibbs free energies in kcal/mol at the stated temperature relative to infinitely separated reactants. The author hypothesis is a proposed route to test, not an answer: do not assume any candidate geometry, ranking, energy or ordered protocol.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use `data/inputs/reaction_system.json`. It defines the exact SMILES, names, neutral charge and singlet multiplicity of triethylphosphine, diethyl 2-cinnamyl-2-(3-(4-nitrophenyl)prop-2-yn-1-yl)malonate (1a), and 4-fluorophenol (2a), plus fluorobenzene, 383.15 K, separated-reactant reference, and an atom-provenance/connectivity definition of the cyclopentenone endpoint. Derive and report the endpoint structure and explain its atom provenance; the endpoint description is not a supplied result structure. You may generate conformers and initial geometries, but may not use the paper/SI or copy result-bearing author geometries. Choose and justify computational methods; the task does not prescribe software or model chemistry.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Define a finite candidate record for each minimum and TS you advance, including candidate identity, geometry provenance, charge/multiplicity, method, conformer coverage, frequency result, and connectivity evidence. Deduplicate candidates by chemically equivalent connectivity and a stated geometric/energy criterion. Advance only minima with a converged optimization and no imaginary frequency, and TS candidates with a converged optimization and exactly one imaginary frequency whose displacement matches the proposed elementary step. Establish each claimed elementary-step connection by IRC or a scientifically justified alternative with explicit endpoint structures. Construct a connected profile from separated reactants to the endpoint, report all investigated candidates and discarded/failed candidates, and quantify method/conformer sensitivity. Completion requires a reproducible connected profile and explicit validation records for every state used in the final conclusion. Stop when additional searches at the stated search strategy produce no new chemically distinct connected states, or report bounded failure with coverage, failed attempts and the missing closure; do not claim uniqueness beyond the investigated scope.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` conforming to `submission_schema.json`. It must include the selected pathway, independently derived endpoint structure plus atom-provenance explanation, per-state energies and validation context, search coverage, and a conclusion. Include either a complete connected profile or a truthful bounded-failure branch; in bounded failure, omit unsupported barrier values and explain the missing closure rather than inventing numbers. Numeric values must be in kcal/mol and identify the reference and temperature. Do not report the paper's values as if independently calculated.
