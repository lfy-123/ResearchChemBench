# Scientific objective

Determine computationally whether the supplied AB and BA Fe5GeTe2 bilayers are opposite switchable polar ferrimagnetic states connected through the supplied AC intermediate. Independently calculate the AB↔BA switching barrier per periodic cell, the signed out-of-plane polarization of AB and BA, and the signed net magnetic moments of AB, BA and AC. The author hypothesis is that interlayer sliding breaks the symmetry relating opposite spin sublattices and couples polarization and moment reversal without changing the A-type AFM Néel-vector direction; test this hypothesis rather than assuming it.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use `data/inputs/AB.vasp`, `BA.vasp`, and `AC.vasp`. Each is a periodic 16-atom Fe10Ge2Te4 bilayer with the explicit 3.969 Å, 3.969 Å, 37.538 Å cell and c-axis vacuum. `magnetic_initialization.json` defines A-type AFM as parallel Fe1–Fe5, parallel Fe6–Fe10, and opposite layer directions. The measured objects are total-energy differences along an AB-to-BA path, out-of-plane polarization relative to the supplied c axis, and total spin moment per cell. You may choose software, exchange-correlation treatment, relaxation settings, path discretization and polarization implementation, but must disclose them. Do not use the paper, SI, general web search, or hidden reference values. This task does not score transport, Curie temperature, or other stackings.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Relax all three supplied structures and report force/energy convergence and cell constraints. Validate the magnetic state by comparing A-type AFM with at least one explicitly described competing ordering (or provide a quantitatively justified equivalent stability check) for at least AB and AC. Construct and optimize a continuous AB-to-BA sliding path that passes through or quantitatively tests AC; report every image/state identity, deduplication or interpolation rule, and the maximum relative energy. Check that AB and BA are symmetry-related in energy within your stated uncertainty, that their polarizations and moments reverse sign, and that AC is nonpolar/compensated within uncertainty. Perform at least one numerical sensitivity check (k-point, cutoff, relaxation, path-image, or equivalent) for each scored observable. Completion requires either converged values for all observables with uncertainty estimates or a bounded-failure report naming the failed calculation, attempted scope, and scientifically justified limitation. Stop when convergence criteria and the path/stability checks are met; if they cannot be met, stop after documenting the bounded resource/search scope and return the limitation branch.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` matching `submission_schema.json`. Include methods, structure identities, convergence evidence, magnetic comparison, path image table, barrier, signed AB/BA polarization, signed AB/BA/AC moments, uncertainties, conclusion and limitations. Include enough per-object evidence to bind each value to AB, BA or AC and enough path context to reproduce the barrier selection. A bounded-failure submission must still provide attempted calculations, available partial observables, failure reason and limitation.
