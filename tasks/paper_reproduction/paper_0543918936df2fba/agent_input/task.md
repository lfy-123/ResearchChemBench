# Scientific objective

Independently test the authors' qualitative hypothesis that substituting Pt into a Ni(111) surface moderates molecular hydrogen binding and makes the adsorbed HMF transition from a parallel ring-bound geometry to a tilted carbonyl-accessible geometry easier. Determine, for the two explicitly defined surfaces, molecular H2 adsorption energies, the relative stability of relaxed parallel and tilted HMF states, and the minimum-energy forward barrier from the parallel state to the tilted state. Do not assume the hypothesis is correct.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

All identities are defined in `data/inputs/system_spec.json`. HMF is neutral closed-shell 5-(hydroxymethyl)furan-2-carbaldehyde with explicit mapped SMILES; H2 is neutral singlet. Surface `Ni111` is an ideal periodic 4×4 three-layer fcc Ni(111) slab. Surface `NiPt111` is the same slab with exactly one Ni atom in the exposed top layer replaced by Pt; all top-layer sites are translationally equivalent in the clean ideal cell. Use one adsorbate per 4×4 surface cell, at least 15 Å vacuum normal to the slab, and fix only the bottom metal layer during adsorbate/slab relaxation. Optimize or otherwise justify the bulk Ni lattice parameter before slab construction and report it.

The measured adsorption energy is `E(slab+H2) - E(clean slab) - E(isolated H2)` in eV at a common electronic-structure level and cell-compatible numerical settings. “Parallel HMF” means the furan-ring plane is initially within 30° of the surface plane and positioned to permit ring π interaction; “tilted HMF” means the ring-plane angle is initially at least 50° and the aldehyde O atom is directed toward a surface metal site. These are generation definitions: final relaxed structures must be classified by their reported ring-plane angle and bonding contacts. The forward barrier is the maximum electronic energy along a validated minimum-energy path from the lowest discovered parallel minimum to the lowest discovered tilted minimum, relative to that parallel minimum. Solvent, MFI, supported clusters, atomic-H adsorption, finite-temperature free energies, reaction rates, and product selectivity are outside the scored model boundary.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Use a defensible periodic electronic-structure method that treats metallic spin polarization and dispersion, and apply the same physical model to both surfaces. For H2, generate and relax distinct intact molecular placements spanning atop, bridge, and hollow neighborhoods and more than one initial orientation; deduplicate final states by adsorption site, H–H integrity, and geometry, and report every distinct relaxed candidate considered. Advance the lowest intact molecular adsorption state on each surface.

For each surface, generate multiple parallel and tilted HMF placements spanning inequivalent adsorbate rotations and local atop/bridge/hollow neighborhoods. Deduplicate relaxed minima using adsorption contacts, ring-plane angle, and energy. Advance the lowest relaxed minimum in each class to a path search. Validate each path by continuous endpoint connectivity and a highest-energy image or saddle with exactly one unstable mode projected predominantly along the parallel↔tilted coordinate, or provide an equally strong path-tangent/force validation if Hessian analysis is impractical. Demonstrate numerical stability of energy differences against at least one tighter setting affecting slab calculations and report the observed changes.

Completion requires, for both named surfaces, a documented candidate inventory, validated lowest parallel and tilted endpoints, a validated connecting path, H2 adsorption energy, HMF relative endpoint energy, forward barrier, and a conclusion about the hypothesis. Stop candidate generation when every required site neighborhood and orientation class has been attempted and a new batch of symmetry-distinct starts produces no new minimum within the method's stated structural and energetic deduplication criteria. Stop path refinement when the reported barrier is stable to the stated path and electronic convergence criteria. If convergence or a validated path cannot be obtained within available resources, terminate as `bounded_failure` only after reporting attempted candidates, diagnostics, the last trustworthy quantities, and the specific limitation.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` matching `submission_schema.json`. It must identify the computational model, bulk lattice parameter, convergence evidence, all H2 and HMF candidates with per-candidate validation context, selected endpoints, per-surface energies in eV, path validation, and a scoped conclusion. Successful completion uses status `complete`; scientifically bounded non-completion uses `bounded_failure` and must not fabricate missing energies or structures.
