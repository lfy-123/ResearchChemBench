# Scientific objective

Independently test the authors' qualitative hypothesis that product thermodynamics supports excluding the mixed-configuration (1S,2R) and (1R,2S) 2-azidocyclohexan-1-ol products from the dominant stereochemical outcomes of cyclohexene-oxide azidolysis. Determine conformer-resolved aqueous relative Gibbs free energies at 298.15 K for all four specified stereoisomers and state whether your validated results support, contradict, or remain inconclusive about that hypothesis. This is a product-state thermochemistry task, not an enzyme transition-state or kinetic calculation.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use `data/inputs/system.json` and `data/inputs/README.md`. They define the atom-mapped connectivity, the four absolute configurations RR, SS, SR, and RS, molecular formula C6H11N3O, total charge 0, singlet multiplicity, aqueous environment, and 298.15 K target temperature. Atom 1 is the hydroxyl-bearing carbon and atom 2 is the adjacent azide-bearing carbon. Treat each stereoisomer as an isolated neutral solute in an achiral aqueous model. Generate your own 3D conformers; no supplied geometry is privileged. Do not model enzyme binding, reaction barriers, rates, or product ratios. Choose and document a defensible electronic-structure, solvation, standard-state, and thermal treatment; the paper's software, model chemistry, hydrated-cluster composition, numerical results, and execution order are not public inputs.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

For each of RR, SS, SR, and RS, generate a reproducible finite conformer set, preserve the specified connectivity and absolute configuration, deduplicate geometrically equivalent candidates, and state generation settings and counts before and after deduplication. Advance conformers using a documented energy-window or population criterion broad enough to retain plausible minima; report the criterion and every advanced candidate with a unique candidate identifier and individual optimization/frequency (or equivalent) validation context. Optimize advanced structures and validate the selected state as a local minimum using harmonic frequencies or a justified equivalent. Apply one consistent free-energy convention to all four stereoisomers, including solvent, temperature, standard state, low-frequency treatment, and any explicit-water treatment. Report the lowest conformer free energy for each named stereoisomer relative to the lowest of all four. Check expected achiral-environment symmetry by comparing RR with SS and SR with RS, and perform at least one independently motivated sensitivity check on conformer coverage or thermochemical/model treatment. The investigation is complete when every stereoisomer has at least one validated minimum, the advancement rule has been exhausted, relative free energies and symmetry checks are available, and the sensitivity check no longer changes the qualitative grouping/order; stop then. If resources prevent that criterion, stop only at a predeclared resource bound and report unvalidated objects and the resulting limits without claiming the hypothesis is resolved.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` matching `submission_schema.json`. It must identify the computational model and reproducibility settings; provide named RR, SS, SR, and RS results with relative free energies in kcal/mol, conformer counts, and stationary-point validation; record symmetry and sensitivity assessments; give a complete ordering/grouping; and state whether the calculations support, contradict, or are inconclusive about the authors' hypothesis. Include limitations, especially the distinction between isolated-product thermodynamics and enzyme-bound selectivity.
