# Scientific objective

Determine, for the defined EcCLC membrane system, the chloride-ion PMF along the pore Z coordinate in the water-containing and water-depleted proton-pathway conditions. Quantify the barriers from S_cen to S_ext and S_int, identify the three binding-site valleys, and test the authors' qualitative hypothesis that proton-pathway water facilitates chloride transport. Independently choose and justify computational methods; the author hypothesis is only a proposed explanation, not an expected numerical answer.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use every field in `data/inputs/system_definition.json` and retrieve PDB 1OTS only through the controlled PDB connector. The system is the EcCLC dimer with chains A and B, POPE membrane, TIP3P water, 0.2 M NaCl, stated protonation, 300 K and 1 atm. The measured coordinate is the chloride Z position relative to a reported protein reference. S_ext, S_cen and S_int mean the external, central and internal binding-site landmarks defined in the input. Do not use the paper/SI or general web as computational inputs. Report model, force field, charge treatment, sampling, and any deliberate deviations.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Build one uniquely identified model for each chain condition and generate a finite set of PMF windows or an equivalent validated free-energy sampling design covering the complete transport interval needed to connect S_ext–S_cen–S_int. Deduplicate overlapping windows/replicates, advance only windows with stable coordinates and adequate sampling, and report the number, ranges and independent seeds. Validate equilibration with a trajectory stability diagnostic, validate PMF convergence with block/replicate uncertainty or an equivalent quantitative test for each condition, and assign valleys using the submitted coordinate data rather than labels alone. The investigation is complete when both conditions have converged profiles spanning both directions from S_cen, all three valleys are resolved, and every reported barrier has an uncertainty. If an endpoint, valley, or validation requirement cannot be completed, submit `status: bounded_failure` with the attempted coverage and condition-specific diagnostics instead of success-only results. Stop when added sampling changes each requested barrier by less than the investigator's predeclared criterion across two successive checks; otherwise stop at the stated resource/time boundary and report the limitation.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` containing status, system/model provenance, PMF profiles or sufficient tabulated points, valley assignments, four barrier estimates with uncertainties, condition-specific convergence/equilibration diagnostics, hydration condition definitions, and a conclusion about whether the qualitative hypothesis is supported. Use the exact public condition IDs `chain_A_hydrated` and `chain_B_dehydrated`, and directions `S_cen_to_S_ext` and `S_cen_to_S_int`. A bounded-failure submission may leave result arrays empty or partial, but must state which endpoint or validation failed and include attempted coverage and diagnostics in `failure_details`; it must not invent numerical results.
