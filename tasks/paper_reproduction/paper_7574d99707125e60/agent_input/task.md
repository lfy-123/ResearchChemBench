# Scientific objective

Determine the S1 fluorescence emission wavelength of the isolated cationic G1 molecule from the supplied 49-atom starting geometry, and test the authors' qualitative hypothesis that the emissive transition is predominantly intramolecular charge transfer within the guest. The measured endpoint is the relaxed-state S1→S0 emission wavelength in nm, together with a defensible electronic-transition assignment.

The file `data/inputs/G1GS.xyz` contains one connected 49-atom C25H21N2O guest component extracted from a larger host–guest starting structure. Only this isolated guest is supplied; its coordinates are a starting geometry, not an isolated excited-state endpoint; use charge +1 and singlet multiplicity unless a chemically justified alternative is explicitly tested and reported. No cucurbituril host, solvent molecule, experimental spectrum, target wavelength, paper method, or preselected excited-state geometry is provided. You may choose software, electronic-structure model, basis, relativistic treatment, solvation approximation, conformer protocol and state-tracking procedure, but must disclose them. # Required scientific validation/investigation

Plan and execute a reproducible ground-state-to-excited-state workflow that reaches an S1 minimum or a clearly justified alternative endpoint and evaluates S1→S0 emission at that endpoint. Demonstrate charge/multiplicity, convergence, state identity and geometry provenance. Validate each optimized stationary point with a Hessian/frequency check or explain a scientifically equivalent validation. Analyze the emitting transition using NTOs, attachment/detachment densities, transition density, or an equivalent orbital-localization diagnostic, identifying donor and acceptor regions and distinguishing intramolecular CT from host--guest or purely local excitation. If the calculation fails or no trustworthy S1 minimum can be established, report the failure, evidence, attempted coverage and limitation rather than inventing a number. Completion requires a converged endpoint, an emission observable or bounded failure explanation, and the requested validation evidence. Stop when the endpoint is validated and the transition analysis is stable under the reported state-tracking/conformer checks; if instability remains, stop with a limitation report and quantify the unresolved alternatives.
