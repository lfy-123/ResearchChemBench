# Scientific objective

Independently test the authors' qualitative hypothesis that the less transition-metal-coordinated oxygen environment is the preferred proton-binding environment in VO2(B). For monoclinic C2/m VO2(B) at H0.25VO2(B), generate and compare plausible proton configurations, optimize them, and determine whether the intermediate composition is energetically favorable.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

Use `data/inputs/vo2b_pristine.cif`, which defines the conventional C2/m VO2(B) cell, its lattice, symmetry, asymmetric V1/V2/O1/O2/O3/O4 identities and fractional coordinates. Use `data/inputs/problem_definition.json`: neutral periodic H0.25VO2(B), two inserted H atoms in the Z=8 cell. Do not use the paper, SI or general web. You may choose software, functional, pseudopotential, spin state, k-point sampling and relaxation settings, but report them. The scored research object is the relaxed candidate site/configuration and its energy relative to the consistently defined pristine and hydrogen references; no transition state or product structure is requested.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Enumerate candidate proton placements from the crystallographically distinct oxygen environments and symmetry-equivalent sites, retaining a unique candidate identity and placement coordinates. Use a finite, stated generation rule; deduplicate candidates by symmetry/geometry and state the resulting coverage. Advance each retained candidate through the same optimization and verify force/energy convergence, cell integrity, composition, charge and absence of atom loss. Compute an internally consistent formation or insertion energy for every advanced candidate and explain the reference convention. Completion requires either a converged comparison of all candidates generated by the stated rule or a bounded-failure report identifying the unresolved candidates and why. Stop when the generation rule is exhausted and all retained candidates have been compared; if resources prevent this, stop at the declared resource boundary and report coverage and limitations.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` conforming to `submission_schema.json`. Include the candidate table, selected candidate identity, energies, convergence/validation evidence, coverage and stopping status, plus a concise conclusion. Include coordinates or structure files for the selected and compared candidates when available. A bounded-failure branch is allowed only if it truthfully records the completed subset and unresolved scope.
