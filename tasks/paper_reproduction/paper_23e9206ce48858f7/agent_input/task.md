# Scientific objective

Test the authors' qualitative proposal that electrochemical reaction of alkyne substrate 1a with diphenyl diselenide proceeds through selenium-centered radical addition, oxidation/cyclization, and selenium-anion capture. Independently plan and perform calculations on the supplied molecular system and report the activation free energies for the three consecutively connected elementary steps, labeled by your own unambiguous state identifiers (TS1-like addition, TS2-like cyclization, TS3-like capture). The measured quantity is a free-energy barrier in kcal/mol from the directly preceding connected state at 298.15 K and 1 M, with THF solvent treatment stated by the submitter.

# Public inputs and scientific boundaries

`data/inputs/chemical_system.json` defines substrate 1a and diphenyl diselenide 2a by name, stereochemical SMILES, formula, neutral charge and singlet multiplicity. It also defines the observed electrochemical selenylation/cyclization channel and the thermodynamic reporting boundary. You may generate conformers, reactive fragments and intermediate/transition-state guesses, but do not treat any paper coordinate block, paper energy, or external paper/SI text as an input. The scored objects are the three connected elementary steps and their barriers, not product yield. A completed result requires each reported TS to have a frequency characterization, an IRC or equivalent two-sided connectivity test, a clearly identified preceding state, and a reproducible energy definition. If a scientifically valid three-step result cannot be obtained after documenting all attempted candidates and coverage, submit the bounded-failure branch with no fabricated barrier values and state what remains unresolved.

# Required scientific validation/investigation

Describe the model chemistry, charge/spin choices, conformer strategy, optimization and frequency procedure, energy refinement/solvation treatment, and barrier formula. Generate and deduplicate plausible structures for each step; retain identities and validation evidence for every advanced candidate. Minima must have no imaginary frequencies and a TS must have one relevant imaginary frequency (or explain a scientifically justified alternative). IRC or an equivalent validated path-following/connection analysis must connect each TS to the stated neighboring states. Report convergence problems, alternative conformers, and sensitivity checks. Do not claim mechanistic support from barriers alone if connectivity or state identity is unresolved.

# Deliverables

Submit `report/results.json` following `submission_schema.json`. The completed branch contains exactly three named step records, each with barrier, unit, preceding/following state identifiers, frequency evidence, connectivity evidence and energy definition, plus methodology, validation summary, conclusion and limitations. The bounded-failure branch identifies failed step(s), preserves attempted candidate and validation records, states what can and cannot be concluded, and gives a stopping rationale; it does not contain success-only numeric step records. Include enough raw-log paths or compact extracted data for an evaluator to audit the claims.
