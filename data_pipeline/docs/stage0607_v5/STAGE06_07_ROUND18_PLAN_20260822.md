# Stage06/07 v5 Round 18 plan — Stage06B recovery budget

## Trigger

The Round 16 targeted retries passed Stage06 input preparation and Stage06A, but both Stage06B
conversions required recovery after an interrupted large write. Configuration supplied
`recovery_max_tool_calls=8` and `autonomous_converter_finalization_reserve=8`, with no converter-
specific recovery override. The orchestrator clamped the reserve to seven and therefore allowed
only one ordinary workspace call on each recovery attempt. The Agent logs explicitly reported that
the hard limit prevented it from reading the recovery context and applying the repair.

This is a generic orchestration-budget defect. It is independent of paper content and separate from
the already-fixed shortened artifact-path receipt.

## Change

- When no explicit `autonomous_converter_recovery_max_tool_calls` is configured, Stage06B recovery
  receives at least its normal `autonomous_converter_max_tool_calls` budget.
- An explicit converter-specific recovery setting still wins.
- Other phases retain their existing recovery budgets.
- Add an end-to-end mock regression showing that an 8-call global recovery default plus an 8-call
  finalization reserve no longer reduces Stage06B to one usable call, and that the recovered
  file-first artifact also accepts the canonicalized receipt path.

## Boundaries

- No scientific fields are edited by code.
- No retry is converted into an approval.
- No paper, molecule, software, or answer special case is added.
- The retry count remains bounded.
