# Stage06/07 v5 Round 17 plan — resource audit/decision closure

## Trigger

In the Round 14 random batch, every scientifically approved task (3/3) was published with
`resource_status=uncertain`. The mandatory routes ranged from six 102–106 atom electronic-structure
branches, through twelve 122–130 atom optimization/Hessian/TDDFT branches, to twenty optimizations
plus eight TDDFT calculations. Stage07 correctly expanded the work and removed unsupported
`feasible` assertions, but then left `remaining_issues=[]` and approved anyway.

This is a repeated Stage06A/Stage07 decision-closure problem. It is not a mechanical-gate defect and
does not justify Stage07B.

## Change

1. Stage06A must expand mandatory branches, system sizes, expensive methods, dependency waves,
   validation reruns and resource limits before selecting a full route.
2. Exact measured timings are not required. A conservative scientific estimate is allowed when its
   assumptions are explicit; a bare branch-count assertion is not evidence.
3. Stage07 resource statuses receive unambiguous semantics:
   - `feasible`: comfortably within policy;
   - `high_cost`: demonstrably within policy but near the limit;
   - `infeasible`: exceeds policy;
   - `uncertain`: no defensible classification.
4. An approved decision cannot carry `uncertain` or `infeasible`. Stage07 must narrow to the most
   important closed feasible subworkflow, or scientifically reject if none exists.

## Boundaries

- No code-side atom-count, method, molecule, runtime, or paper rule.
- No deterministic scientific cost gate.
- Missing software remains non-blocking and cannot justify scope downgrade.
- A scientific rejection for genuine resource non-closure is valid; approval rate is not the target.
- Stage07B remains disabled because the issue requires scientific judgment rather than narrow
  transport repair.
